# Feature Requests

You are free to suggest any feature. If it is feasible, given enough time, it will be added.

---

## How to Submit a Request

The best way to submit a feature request is through the community channels listed on the [Contact](contact.md) page.

---

## What Makes a Good Request

A good feature request includes:

- **What you want** -- a clear description of the feature
- **How it should work** -- the specific mechanics and behavior
- **Why it is useful** -- what problem it solves or what it improves

> **:bulb: Detail Matters**
>
> The more detail you provide, the better. Requests that include specific mechanics and expected behavior are much more likely to be implemented quickly. Vague requests require the developer to research the mechanics first, which delays implementation.

---

## What Makes a Bad Request

Requests that are vague or require extensive research on the developer's part take longer to implement. For example:

- "Add support for X encounter" with no details about what the encounter does
- "Make the bot better at Y" without explaining what specifically needs to change
