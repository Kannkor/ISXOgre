# Settings 2

The Settings 2 tab is an extension of the Settings tab, providing additional on/off toggles for various bot features.

## Row 1: Event & Combat Controls

### Event Controls

- **Enable Chat Events** - Toggles the Chat Events tab functionality on or off
- **Enable Spawn Events** - Activates the Spawn Events tab
- **Enable Despawn Events** - Activates the Despawn Events tab

### Combat & Gameplay

- **HO's** - Activates the Heroic Opportunity wheel without reordering spells
- **Crowd Control** - Enables crowd control mechanics
- **Disable Ability Collision Checks** - Allows targeting and casting through obstacles
- **Disable Merc Support** - Disables mercenary support
- **Accept Raid Ready Checks** - Automatically confirms raid leader readiness checks
- **Accept Dungeon is Ready** - Automatically accepts Dungeon Finder group invitations

### Ability Management

- **Disable Pre-Cast Tab** - Disables all pre-cast abilities
- **Disable Post-Cast Tab** - Disables all post-cast abilities
- **Disable Ogre Fly** - Disables Ogre Fly functionality

### Server & Gameplay Options

- **Enable PVP** - Allows player combat on PVP servers
- **Do not auto accept quests** - Prevents automatic quest acceptance
- **Do not check Research on load** - Skips research checks when loading (useful for TLE servers)
- **Do not check Mount Training on load** - Skips mount training progress verification on load
- **Skip Cutscenes** - Automatically ends cinematics/cutscenes

## Row 2: Additional Options

### Invisibility Management

- **Cancel Invis After Combat** - Removes invisibility after combat ends
- **No Invis Casting** - Prevents casting while invisible

### Pet & Interface Options

- **Enable Pets in Dropdowns** - Shows pet names in selection dropdown menus
- **Do not send pet to attack** - Prevents automatic pet engagement

### Quest & Movement

- **Enable Quest Compare** - Compares quests across sessions
- **Cast while moving** - Allows spell casting during movement
