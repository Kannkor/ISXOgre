## Anathraxxis

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight with floor spew mechanics that require repositioning away from the danger zone.

### What the Module Does

**Spew Avoidance:**

- When "the floor cracks and oozes beneath" is announced, determines the spew target location
- Chooses between two safe positions, picking whichever is farther from the spew
- Repositions the raid via campspot

### Player Notes

- Positioning is automatic based on spew location detection
