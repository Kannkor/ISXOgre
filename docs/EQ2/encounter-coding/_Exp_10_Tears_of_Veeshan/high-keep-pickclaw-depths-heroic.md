## Bolgin Serilis

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight where the group must stop attacking during the boss's potion drinking phase.

### What the Module Does

**Potion Phase:**

- When Bolgin pops a cork, all characters stop offensive actions, cancel auto-attack, cancel spellcasts, and send pets to back off
- After a 12-second countdown, offensive actions resume and the boss is re-targeted

### Player Notes

- The attack pause is fully automated
