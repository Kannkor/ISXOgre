## Available Setups

| Boss | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [General](#general) | Close quarters joust for non-fighters |

---

## General

Setup command: `Set up for General`

### Overview

A fight where non-fighters must joust away when they receive the Close Quarters detriment.

### What the Module Does

**Close Quarters Joust:**

- Monitors the Close Quarters detriment on non-fighters
- When detected, non-fighters automatically move to the joust spot
- When the detriment clears, they return to their normal position
- Fighters are exempt from jousting

### Player Notes

- Fighters stay in place; only non-fighters need to move
