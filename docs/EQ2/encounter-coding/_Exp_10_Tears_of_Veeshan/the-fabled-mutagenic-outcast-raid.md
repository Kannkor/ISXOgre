## The Mutagenic Outcast

Setup command: `Set up for Mutagenic` (also accepts `Set up for Outcast`)

### Overview

A fight with curse cleansing via sludge pools and alternating camp positions.

### What the Module Does

**Curse-to-Sludge Cleansing:**

- When the Mutagenic Contagion curse is detected, waits for a Mutagenic Sludge to spawn
- Jousts out and moves to the sludge's location to cleanse the curse by touching it
- Returns to camp position after the curse clears

**Alternating Positions:**

- Two raid spot positions alternate as sludge pools grow
- When a sludge spawns within 5 meters of the current position, automatically swaps to the other position

**AE Timer:**

- Mutagenic Cloud timer (25/38 seconds)

### Player Notes

- The raid leader runs a pre-cure timer for Mutagenic Cloud (45 seconds)
