## The Three Princes

Setup command: `Set up for Prince` (also accepts `Set up for Three`)

### Overview

A positioning setup with a special spot for enchanters in raid group 1.

### What the Module Does

**Positioning:**

- Enchanters in raid group 1 are sent to a separate enchanter-specific position
- All other characters go to the main group position

### Player Notes

- Positioning only, with enchanter-specific placement
