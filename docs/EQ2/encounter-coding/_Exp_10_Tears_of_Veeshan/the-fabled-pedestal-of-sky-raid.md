## Siyamak / Barakah (Dragons)

Setup command: `Set up for Dragon` (also accepts `Set up for Siyamak` or `Set up for Barakah`)

### Overview

A fight with day and night curse mechanics requiring players to run to the opposing dragon.

### What the Module Does

**Day/Night Curse Navigation:**

- Two curse types: Night curse and Day curse
- Night curse: automatically moves to Barakah of the Day's location
- Day curse: automatically moves to Siyamak of the Night's location
- Jousts out, moves to the correct dragon, and returns when the curse clears

**Group-Based Positioning:**

- Raid groups 1 and 3 share one set of tank/raid positions
- Raid groups 2 and 4 share another set

### Player Notes

- Positioning is determined by raid group number
