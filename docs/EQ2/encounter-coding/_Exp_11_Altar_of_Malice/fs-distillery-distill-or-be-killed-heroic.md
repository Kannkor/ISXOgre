| Boss | Description |
|------|-------------|

---

## The Red Corsair

Setup command: `Set up for Corsair` (also accepts `Set up for The Red Corsair`)

### Overview

A fight where looted cannonballs must be loaded and fired from a cannon.

### What the Module Does

**Cannonball Automation:**

- When a player loots a cannonball from a corsair cannoneer, the module takes over
- If the player is not near the cannon, automatically navigates to the cannon location
- Loads the cannon, fires it, targets the boss, clicks the boss, and repeats while cannonballs remain

### Player Notes

- The player must manually place the cannon and assign cannonball loot
- Positions the group behind a vat
