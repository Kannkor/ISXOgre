## Mo'iana

Setup command: `Set up for Moiana` (also accepts `Set up for Mo'iana` or `Set up for Ravenous`)

### Overview

A fight requiring spread positioning and curse management.

### What the Module Does

**Spread Positioning:**

- Spreads the group into 6 positions radiating from a center point (approximately 13-15 meters apart)
- Priest 1 is placed in the center, all others assigned by sort order

**Curse Callout:**

- Monitors for curse detriment and calls out via group say when detected

### Player Notes

- Use MCP Special to get on the raptors
