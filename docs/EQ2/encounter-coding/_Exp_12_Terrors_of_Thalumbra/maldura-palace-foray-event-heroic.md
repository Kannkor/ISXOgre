# Maldura: Palace Foray [Event Heroic]

**Expansion:** Terrors of Thalumbra (Exp 12)

This zone has 2 boss encounters with OgreBot automation modules.

## Available Setups

| Boss | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [Dreadmaw](#dreadmaw) | Auto-uses wolf whistle |
| [Dreadtusk](#dreadtusk) | Auto-uses boar horn on impale |

---

## Dreadmaw

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight where players must use a wolf whistle when Dreadmaw stares at them.

### What the Module Does

**Wolf Whistle:**

- When Dreadmaw stares in your direction, automatically uses "a wolf whistle" item after a 3-second delay

### Player Notes

- You must have "a wolf whistle" in your inventory

---

## Dreadtusk

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight where players must use a boar horn to counter the Impale mechanic.

### What the Module Does

**Boar Horn:**

- Monitors for the Impale II detriment on the group
- When detected and at least 3 seconds have elapsed, automatically uses "a boar horn" from inventory

### Player Notes

- You must have "a boar horn" in your inventory
