## Auto Stand-Up

Setup is automatic.

### Overview

The zone has knockdown mechanics that cause players to fall to the ground.

### What the Module Does

**Auto Stand:**

- When your character falls to the ground, the module automatically issues a stand command to get back up

### Player Notes

- This is a quality-of-life feature that works automatically throughout the zone
