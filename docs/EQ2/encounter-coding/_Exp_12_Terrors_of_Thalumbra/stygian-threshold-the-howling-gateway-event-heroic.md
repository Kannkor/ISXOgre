## All Encounters

Setup is automatic.

### Overview

A zone-wide mechanic where players' spells or combat abilities are periodically purged. Fighters automatically intercept the targeted player.

### What the Module Does

**Purge Intercept:**

- When a player's spells or combat abilities are about to be purged, all fighters attempt to intercept the targeted player

### Player Notes

- Use `Special_ZoneSpecific` in MCP to click the orb at the same time as your group
