## Chel'Drak the Ancient Lord

Setup command: `Set up for Chel`

### Overview

A raid fight with basic positioning and an automatic target bug workaround.

### What the Module Does

**Positioning:**

- Fighters are placed at the tank spot, everyone else at the raid spot

**Target Bug Check:**

- Continuously monitors for and corrects the known EQ2 target bug where a character's target gets stuck

### Player Notes

- Fighters have a separate tank spot
