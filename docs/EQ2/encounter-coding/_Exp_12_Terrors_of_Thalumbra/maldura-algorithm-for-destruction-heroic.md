## Black Bolt

### Overview

A fight where players must choose which portal to enter.

### What the Module Does

**Portal Selection:**

- Stand next to the portal you want to go through, then press `Special_ZoneSpecific` in MCP

### Player Notes

- Campspot your group first, then position next to the desired portal before pressing the button
