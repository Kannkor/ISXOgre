## Yothshaval

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight where Yothshaval is periodically protected by three adds. The module prevents the group from wasting offensive abilities on the protection adds while they are shielded.

### What the Module Does

**Protection Mechanic:**

- When Yothshaval becomes protected, offensive abilities are disabled against Shield of Yothshaval, Sustainer of Yothshaval, and Watcher of Yothshaval
- When the protection subsides, offensive abilities are re-enabled on those targets
- If any of the three adds despawn while Yothshaval is still alive, offense is also re-enabled

### Player Notes

- This module requires Elite access
