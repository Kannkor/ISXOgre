## Arreken Skyward

Setup command: `Set up for Arreken`

### Overview

A fight with red text jousting between two positions.

### What the Module Does

**Red Text Joust:**

- On tail thrash or massive bite announcements, toggles the camp spot between the main raid spot and joust spot
- Calls joust out to interrupt casts during transitions

### Player Notes

- Standard two-position toggle joust
