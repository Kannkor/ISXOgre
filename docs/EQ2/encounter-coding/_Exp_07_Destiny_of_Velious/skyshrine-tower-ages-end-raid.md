## Roehn Theer

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight with curse-based repositioning, a knockback timer, and add management for group 2.

### What the Module Does

**Curse Repositioning:**

- When cursed, shifts the camp spot 15 meters away from the group to avoid splash damage
- Returns to normal position when the curse clears

**Knockback Timer:**

- Godly Draw — 45-second HUD countdown

**Primal Force Add Management (Group 2):**

- When a Growing Primal Force spawns and Roehn Theer is above 20% HP, group 2 members move to the add's location and force-target it
- On add despawn, checks for remaining primal forces or returns to the saved camp spot

### Player Notes

- Curse mechanic automatically maintains safe distance from other players
