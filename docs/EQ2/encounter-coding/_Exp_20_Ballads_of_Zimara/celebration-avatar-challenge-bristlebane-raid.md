## Fizzlethorpe Bristlebane

Setup is automatic when engaged. Or you can use `Obj_OgreMCP:PasteButton[SetUpFor__,SetUpFor]` to do the set up immediately.

### Overview

A fight where the boss pranks players, requiring them to find and click a gigglegibber prankster NPC to interrupt the prank.

### What the Module Does

**Pranks on YOU!:**

- Detects the "Pranks on YOU!" detriment on the player
- Automatically moves the player to the gigglegibber prankster NPC
- Clicks to interrupt the prank
- Tracks prankster movement during the fight

**TTS Alerts:**

- Text-to-Speech alerts announce when a player has been pranked

### Player Notes

- Prank interrupting is handled automatically
- TTS alerts help coordinate the group
