## Katakir the Cruel

Setup is automatic when engaged. Or you can use `Obj_OgreMCP:PasteButton[SetUpFor__,SetUpFor]` to do the set up immediately.

### Overview

A fight with Heroic Opportunity management and automatic dispelling of the named's Purify buff.

### What the Module Does

**Dispell Management:**

- Automatically dispells the Purify buff from the named

**Heroic Opportunity (HO) Management:**

- Sets up HO automation for the fight
- Fighters are designated to start HOs when Iron Will is detected on the named

### Player Notes

- HOs and dispells are handled automatically
- Fighters will initiate HOs during Iron Will phases

## Metalloid

Setup is automatic when engaged. Or you can use `Obj_OgreMCP:PasteButton[SetUpFor__,SetUpFor]` to do the set up immediately.

### Overview

A fully automated fight with iron pillar jousting, Bulwark of Order management, and NPC cast monitoring.

### What the Module Does

**Iron Pillar Jousting:**

- Monitors for iron pillars spawning near the group
- When a pillar appears too close, jousts the group away to a safe position

**Bulwark of Order:**

- Fighters automatically cast Bulwark of Order during the fight

**NPC Cast Monitoring:**

- Monitors the named's casts and responds to specific abilities automatically

**Heroic Opportunity (HO) Management:**

- Sets up HO automation for the fight

### Player Notes

- The fight is fully automated -- follow the jousting when iron pillars appear
- Fighters should have Bulwark of Order available
