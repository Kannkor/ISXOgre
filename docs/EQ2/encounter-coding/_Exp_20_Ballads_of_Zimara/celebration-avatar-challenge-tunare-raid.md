## Tunare

Setup is automatic when engaged. Or you can use `Obj_OgreMCP:PasteButton[SetUpFor__,SetUpFor]` to do the set up immediately.

### Overview

A fight with the Eye of the Lurker detriment requiring positional movement, plus lurker add management for fighters.

### What the Module Does

**Eye of the Lurker:**

- Detects the Eye of the Lurker detriment on the player
- Automatically moves the player to the closest safe position (front or back of the named) based on their archetype

**Add Targeting:**

- Fighters automatically target lurker adds

### Player Notes

- The Eye of the Lurker positioning is handled automatically
- Fighters should focus on lurker adds when they spawn
