## Mastersmith Naghig Dakgu

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight with animated flame adds and interactive items that must be clicked.

### What the Module Does

**Add Spawn Timer:**

- When an animated flame add spawns, starts a 45-second HUD countdown for the next add wave
- Text-to-speech announcement at 10 seconds remaining

**Item Auto-Click:**

- When the Mastersmith's hammer, anvil, or metal spawns, announces the item via text-to-speech
- Starts a 20-second HUD countdown for the next item spawn
- Automatically double-clicks any of the three Mastersmith items when within range

### Player Notes

- Items are clicked automatically when close enough
