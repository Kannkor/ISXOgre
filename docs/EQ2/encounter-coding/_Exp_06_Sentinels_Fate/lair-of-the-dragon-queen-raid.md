# Lair of the Dragon Queen [Raid]

**Expansion:** Sentinel's Fate (Exp 06)

This zone has 3 boss encounters with OgreBot automation modules.

## Available Setups

| Boss | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [Toxxulia](#toxxulia) | Dragon swallow rescue (Coercer item use) |
| [Zaos](#zaos) | Automated crush rescue |
| [Tuluun](#tuluun) | Noxious cure assist via crystal pool |

---

## Toxxulia

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight where the dragon swallows raid members who must be rescued.

### What the Module Does

**Dragon Swallow Rescue:**

- When a player is swallowed, starts a 60-second HUD timer
- Coercers automatically use the "Staff of Calling" item to summon a crystal, then apply "Recall lost party member" on it to rescue the swallowed player

### Player Notes

- Coercers require "Staff of Calling" inventory item

---

## Zaos

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight where the boss crushes raid members who need to be freed.

### What the Module Does

**Crush Rescue:**

- When a raid member is being crushed by Zaos' foot, automatically applies "Help free raidmember" on the boss

### Player Notes

- Rescue interaction is fully automated

---

## Tuluun

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight where noxious-afflicted players must be led to a crystal pool.

### What the Module Does

**Noxious Cure Assist:**

- Bards and Enchanters scan raid members for noxious debuffs
- When a debuffed player is found within 20 meters, applies "Lead me to the crystal pool" on them to cleanse the noxious

### Player Notes

- Only Bards and Enchanters perform the cleansing interaction
