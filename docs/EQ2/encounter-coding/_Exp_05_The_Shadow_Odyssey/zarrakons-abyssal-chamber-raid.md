## Zarrakon

Setup is automatic when engaged.

### Overview

A fight with dual portal mechanics and add targeting based on debuffs.

### What the Module Does

**Red/Blue Portal Jousting:**

- When the red portal debuff is detected, jousts out and repositions to the red portal location via campspot
- When the blue portal debuff is detected, jousts out and repositions to the blue portal location via campspot
- Announces which portal the character is heading to
- Clears campspot when the debuff drops

**Add Targeting:**

- When the add debuff is detected, forces targeting to "Mystmyrian Wraith"
- Clears targeting when the debuff drops

### Player Notes

- Portal movement and add targeting operate independently
