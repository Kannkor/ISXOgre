# How to Cancel a Subscription

> **:angry: Fair Warning**
>
> First off, you're going to make an Ogre very angry if you cancel.

---

## Method 1: Through OgreGaming.com (Recommended)

1. Visit [ogregaming.com](https://ogregaming.com/)
2. Click on **Subscriptions**
3. Click the **Unsubscribe** button at the bottom
4. You will be directed to PayPal to view your active subscriptions
5. Choose which subscriptions to cancel

> **:bulb: Tip**
>
> Using the Unsubscribe button is much easier than the manual method below.

---

## Method 2: Through PayPal (Manual)

1. Log into [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/)
2. Navigate to your recurring payments
3. Select the subscription you wish to cancel
