# Pricing

ISXOgre is available as a subscription that includes both OgreBot and OgreCraft.

---

## Subscription Plan

### Ogre Combo Pack (OgreBot + OgreCraft)

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Monthly cost** | $13.34/month |
| **Minimum purchase** | 3 months ($40.00) |
| **Payment method** | PayPal |

!!! note "Why 3-Month Minimum?"
    The minimum is 3 months because PayPal charges a flat fee plus a percentage on each transaction. Bundling into larger payments keeps costs reasonable.

---

## What Is Included

Your subscription gives you:

- **OgreBot** -- Full adventure bot with all features
- **OgreCraft** -- Full crafting bot
- **All future updates** -- Weekly updates and bug fixes
- **20+ additional tools** -- Harvesting, mapping, inventory management, collection manager, and more
- **Personal use** -- Use on up to **5 different computers** within a 24-hour period
- **Unlimited sessions** -- No limit on how many EQ2 sessions per computer
- **Up to 2 IP addresses** -- Use from up to 2 different IP addresses within 24 hours

---

## What Is NOT Included

- **Support is volunteer-based** -- Community members help on Discord, IRC, and forums, but support is not guaranteed
- **No custom development** -- Feature requests are welcome but not guaranteed
- **No paid assistance** -- There is no premium support tier

---

## Prerequisites

!!! warning "Required First"
    You must have valid, working subscriptions to both **InnerSpace** and **ISXEQ2** before purchasing ISXOgre. These are separate products that ISXOgre depends on.

---

## How to Subscribe

Visit [ogregaming.com](https://ogregaming.com/) to set up your account and payment.
