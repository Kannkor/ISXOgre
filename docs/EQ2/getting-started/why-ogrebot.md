# Why OgreBot?

OgreBot is a UI-driven, fully customizable adventure bot for EverQuest 2. Whether you are boxing a full group or just want a helping hand on your main character, OgreBot handles combat, buffing, healing, and much more -- all without requiring you to write a single line of code.

---

## Full UI Control

OgreBot gives you control over nearly every aspect of the bot through an intuitive, in-game user interface. You do not need to edit config files, write scripts, or learn a programming language. Everything from ability priorities to healing thresholds can be configured by clicking checkboxes, adjusting sliders, and reordering lists.

!!! tip "No Coding Required"
    Unlike many automation tools, OgreBot is designed so that you never need to look at a piece of code to get up and running.

---

## Automatic Spell Management

One of OgreBot's standout features is its automated spell export system. You run the export once per character, and OgreBot learns all of your abilities, AAs, and spells. When new spells are added, a level cap increases, or new AA lines are introduced, your new abilities are **automatically imported** the next time you export -- no manual spell list editing required.

---

## Frequent Updates

OgreBot is actively maintained. Updates generally come on a weekly basis, and bug fixes are typically released within 24 hours of being discovered. When you launch ISXOgre, files are automatically updated so you are always running the latest version.

---

## Works Across All Classes

OgreBot does not use separate class files. Most abilities work universally across all classes, with class-specific options appearing only when relevant. This means less setup and fewer things that can break when game changes happen.

---

## Crowd Control Built In

OgreBot includes full mezzing (crowd control) support out of the box. Enchanters and other CC-capable classes can automatically manage adds without any extra configuration beyond enabling the feature.

---

## Quick Setup

OgreBot is designed to be a plug-and-play experience. After installation, you can be up and running within minutes. Every class ships with a sample profile that gives you a working starting point, so you can jump into gameplay immediately and fine-tune settings as you learn.

---

## Active Community

OgreBot has an active community on Discord, IRC, and the forums. Other users are happy to share profiles, tips, and encounter strategies. You are never on your own when trying to figure something out.

---

## What Can OgreBot Do?

Here is a quick overview of the major features included with your subscription:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Combat Automation** | Manages your cast stack, ability rotations, and combat arts |
| **Healing and Curing** | Automatic healing, curing, and resurrection |
| **Buffing** | Maintains buffs on yourself and group members |
| **Crowd Control** | Automatic mezzing and CC management |
| **Assist and Targeting** | Configurable assist targets and auto-targeting |
| **Following** | Automatic follow with customizable distance |
| **Looting** | Configurable loot handling and distribution |
| **Instance Controller** | Automated boss fight mechanics for supported zones |
| **Profile System** | Save, load, and share configurations across characters |
| **OgreCraft** | Full crafting automation (included with subscription) |
| **Harvesting** | Automated resource gathering |
| **Inventory Management** | Bag sorting, item management, and more |

!!! note "All Included"
    OgreBot and OgreCraft are bundled together in a single subscription. You get access to all tools and features, plus all future updates.
