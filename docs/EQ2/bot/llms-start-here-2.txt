# What's new

What changed in OgreBot, release by release, newest first. Every release listed here was published to the patcher.

A release is named by the day it was published and its number that day: **2026.10.16.005** is the fifth release on October 16, 2026. Releases with nothing you would notice are not listed, so the numbers can skip.

Dates and times are US Eastern time (EST in winter, EDT in summer).

## 2026.11.08.003 {#r2026-11-08-003}

*Sunday, November 8, 2026 at 5:21 PM EST*

- **Changed** · Bot: **An item row only uses that item's own clicky.** A Cast Stack row that names a worn item with no clicky of its own no longer clicks it, and the console says so once. If an adornment on it is the clicky, use an Equipped or Adornment row, which check the adornment's buff.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **-UseItemOnPlayer waits its turn.** -UseItemOnPlayer was lost if anything was on the cast bar at that moment. It now joins the queue like -UseItem and goes as soon as the bar is free.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **CoV waits for the cast bar.** A CoV command given while something was casting skipped the orb and carried on with no porter. It now waits until the cast bar is free, and the rotation holds off until it is done.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Item examines get longer to answer, and a failure says why.** An item examine now waits up to 6 seconds for its window instead of 2, so a slow one is read and saved instead of being left open on screen. If it still fails, the log says whether the window never opened or opened without a readable item.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Worn items in the Cast Stack are found, and a skipped item says exactly why.** A worn clicky on a Cast Stack row without Require Item Equipped now fires: every item row looks at your gear first, then your bags. The Casting debug log now says exactly why an item was skipped: not found, not equipped, adornment missing, nothing in that slot, or on its reuse timer. It also says where the item was found.

## 2026.11.08.002 {#r2026-11-08-002}

*Sunday, November 8, 2026 at 5:40 AM EST*

- **New** · Bot: **CancelCasting takes -UnlessCasting and -CastAbilityOnNPC.** CancelCasting now understands -UnlessCasting followed by an ability, the same as naming that ability to spare it, and -CastAbilityOnNPC followed by an ability and a mob, which queues that cast and then cancels. A line using either one used to cancel nothing.
- **New** · Bot: **Pass LORE items you already have to your group.** A new Leader Only box on the Loot tab, Pass LORE items I already have to the group, hands a LORE item you already have to a group member who does not have one. The bot asks the group once per chest, and only members whose bot answers can be given one. It does nothing in a raid, and the instance controller default setup ticks it.
- **New** · Windows: **MCP buttons can ask before they send.** Tick Ask when pressed in Build command, and pressing the button opens a small window with a field for each of its values, the cursor already in the first: Tab or Enter moves on, Enter on the last sends, Esc cancels, and blank keeps what the button says. Ready-made in Build command adds the old input buttons back - Assist, Ogre Follow, OgreFollow Off, CampSpot, Door, Use Item, Travel and more - and a new MCP has an Inputs page of them. A key assigned to an asking button opens the window too.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Close loot window when done is its own box.** Loot everything no longer closes the loot window, so anything the bot cannot take waits for you. The new Close loot window when done box closes a Leader Only window once the bot has finished with it. The instance controller default setup ticks it, so a run loots each chest and moves on.
- **Changed** · Instance Controller: **Queue a zone by name from one root.** ogre ic queue add custom followed by a zone name now looks for that zone in custom only, the same for test and default, so ogre ic queue add custom ${Zone.Name} works in a script. Before, anything after the root word was read as a file path and refused as no such zone file. A path ending in .json still queues that file.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **"Trakanon's Lair now follows the Raid Options box".** Trakanon's Lair is a raid, but its name has no [Raid] in it, so its fight was switched on by Enable Heroic Setups. It now follows Raid Options, like every other raid.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **"Trakanon's Lair: the hourglass goes to the right spot".** On Trakanon in Trakanon's Lair, players with the hourglass now go out to the proper spot and come back to their own spot when it falls off. Fighters and everyone else now have separate spots. Before, the fight used older positions, so the hourglass sent people to the raid's spot and fighters shared a spot with the raid.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Cast Stack target lists keep up with your group.** A group member who was still zoning in, or in another zone, was left out of the Cast Stack target list, and stayed out until the group changed again. Every member is listed by name now, wherever they are.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **CastAbilityOnNPC waits for its target, and stops if the mob is not there.** -CastAbilityOnNPC and OgreBotAPI.Abilities:CastOnNPC now target the mob, wait until it is your target, cast, and put your target back. Before, the cast went out at once and landed on whatever was selected at that moment, and a mob that could not be found still got a cast at your current target. As in the old bot, nothing is cast when the mob is missing or the ability is not ready.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Items no longer get Recovering after a spell.** An item was used the moment the previous spell finished, so the game answered Recovering and the item only went off when nothing else was casting. Every item use now waits until 0.8 seconds after the last cast, whether it comes from the Cast Stack, -UseItem, consumables, a fight file or anything else, and nothing else can take the cast bar meanwhile.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Leader Only loot no longer loops on an item it cannot take.** A chest with several copies of a LORE key kept the bot trying the same keys again and again until someone closed the window. An item that cannot be looted now gets one try and is then left in the chest, and a LORE item you already have is not tried at all. If a Smart Loot hand-out does not go through, the item goes to the next member who wants it.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **The quest helper no longer clicks zone doors.** A toon that arrived next to a zone teleporter with the quest helper on clicked it and zoned straight back out. The quest helper now skips any object whose name has the word zone, teleporter or exit, as well as everything on its ignore list. Every hail and click it makes now goes in the log file with the zone.

## 2026.11.07.001 {#r2026-11-07-001}

*Saturday, November 7, 2026 at 8:06 PM EST*

- **New** · Navigation: **Put a point in the middle of a path, or drag it to a new place.** The Paths window can now insert a point above the selected one, and you can drag a point to move it, so a change in the middle no longer means rebuilding the path. Scripts have OgreBotAPI.Paths:InsertPoint and MovePoint. The Edit button beside the Auto-Hunt path picker opens the picked path here, and so do `ogre paths` with a path name and ISXOgreEQ2.Paths:Open with the name.
- **New** · Console: **Chat commands have an ogre command.** `ogre chatcmd` starts the router that runs the commands your own characters send you in chat, and `ogre end chatcmd` stops it. It stays off until you start it. `ogre chatcmd status`, `debug`, `reloadauth` and `test` are the `ISXOgreEQ2.ChatCmd` methods of those names.
- **New** · Other: **A session can say exactly which build it runs.** `${ISXOgreEQ2.BuildHash}` and `${ISXOgreEQ2.CoreBuildHash}` give a fingerprint of the two files this session loaded, and the session log's first adapter line shows both. Two sessions with the same values run the same build.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Auto-Hunt brings the group along and camps it at your spot.** Switching Auto-Hunt on now tells the rest of your group (toons on this PC) to follow you. With Campspot or CS-JO-JI ticked, each pull camps the whole group at your spot, not wherever each toon stood, and nothing more is sent until no mob is left; then they are let go and follow you again. Turning Auto-Hunt off or pausing the bot mid-fight lets them go too, and a toon too far for the camp to pull in is named in the console.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Auto-Hunt holds the whole group for a pull.** The Campspot and CS-JO-JI boxes of Auto-Hunt only moved the toon running it. Now the whole group sets a camp spot for the pull and is let go afterwards, and with CS-JO-JI the casters stay at their camp spot while the rest may go in.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Auto-Hunt only walks.** Auto-Hunt could fly between points on a flying mount, then stand in the air unable to pull. It now only walks, a toon in the air lands first, and a point no walking route reaches ends the walk with a line naming it. A path you start yourself may still fly.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Cast, item and SpewStat events reach your other sessions through OC.** `OgreEvent_OthersOnAbilityCast`, `OgreEvent_OthersOnAbilityCastCompleted`, `OgreEvent_OthersOnItemUse` and `OgreEvent_SpewStats` now reach your other sessions through the Ogre console hub instead of a `relay`, and MCP layouts, MCP key binds and movement key binds sync the same way. The same sessions get them, with the same arguments, each one exactly as sent. A session that is zoning or at character select gets them when it reaches the world, in order, instead of at once.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Classic's Tradeskill Apprentice script is not coming back.** `ogre tsapp` and its manager, `ogre tsam` / `ogre tsappm`, were listed as planned for the Task Runner. They have been dropped instead. Delete those lines from your scripts: nothing replaces them.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Two old Classic content scripts are retired.** Classic's `ogre boz_dw2_korrosk` and `ogre vov_tstracker` were listed as not ported yet. They have been retired instead. Delete those lines from your scripts: nothing replaces them.
- **Changed** · Windows: **Settings that do nothing are now greyed.** In the OgreBot window, a setting that does nothing is now greyed, and hovering it says which kind it is: not built yet, or does nothing by design. It still takes a click and still saves. The guide also now says the Auto Hunt box is a real on/off switch, which it has been for a while.
- **Changed** · Windows: **Temp Adornments always apply.** Applying a temp adornment profile now puts every adornment on, even on a piece that already has it with time left, so you can top up before a long fight. The overwrite tick box and the overwrite argument of `-TempAdorns` and `OgreBotAPI.TempAdorns:Apply` are gone.
- **Changed** · Navigation: **A walk waits when something else has the toon.** A saved path used to end when the toon died, or when something else was moving it. It now waits and goes on from the same point: through a fight, until a res or a revive in the same zone, through a movement block, and through the Pause of the Nav window. A slow new route no longer reads as stuck.
- **Changed** · Navigation: **Changing a path from a script takes a for-who.** OgreBotAPI.Paths:AddPoint, RemovePoint, Clear and Delete now take a for-who first, so a line that reaches six boxes can change the path once. Two boxes saving at the same moment no longer lose an edit.
- **Changed** · Console: **ChatCmd:On no longer warns about classic's router.** `ISXOgreEQ2.ChatCmd:On` and `ogre chatcmd` no longer say that OgreBot Classic's router may still be running: Classic and the new bot refuse each other at load, so the two cannot share a session.
- **Changed** · Other: **A script's cast monitoring list now ends with the zone.** Mobs added with `SetCastMonitoring[...,-watch,"<mob>"]` or `ogre npccast watch` now last the zone, as they did in classic: they end at the next zone or when NPC Cast Monitoring is unticked, together with a script's radius. Zone and fight-file watches are not touched. `-radius` on its own and `ogre npccast radius` now tick NPC Cast Monitoring too, as `-watch` does, and the box stays ticked after the zone.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Auto-Hunt does not pull while you fly.** Auto-Hunt could pick a mob and try to pull it while you were flying, and its pull hold could stop a walk you started yourself in the air, where no pull can be cast. Now it pulls nothing in the air (no pull, target, camp or safety hold), a pull it was working ends, and one console line says so as you take off. Its own walk lands first and then pulls, and a walk you started yourself flies on.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Auto-Hunt holds steady on the mob it picks.** Auto-Hunt's line-of-sight check could flip from one frame to the next on a mob standing in plain view, so the hunt kept stopping and starting, and with Campspot on the group was camped and let go many times a second. It now looks from your eye height to the mob's, the same check your pull ability has to pass, and a picked mob stays picked until a different answer holds for 0.3 seconds. An empty Pull Abilities list now says so once in the console.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Auto-Hunt stops walking when something attacks it.** Auto-Hunt kept walking its path if an attacker was outside the Scan radius and Check Aggro was off. It now stops, fights and carries on at the same point, by the rule the Travel window uses, and a path you start yourself does the same.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Broadcast guild talk works again.** The Broadcast guild talk through OgreConsole box on the Setup tab did nothing, because the code behind it only ran after `ogre chatcmd`. Tick it on one character and guild and officer chat reaches OgreConsole on all your sessions, with no `ogre chatcmd` needed.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Examine finds an item with a coloured name.** Examine and Examine All missed an item whose name carries a colour code, because the backslash of the code was lost on the way to the game. They find it now, and Examine finds it whether or not you type the code.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **LogToonIn reaches a toon a zone dropped to character select.** When a zone drops a toon to character select, `oc !c -LogToonIn MyToon MyToon` now logs it back in: at character select or the login screen a box answers to the toon it last played, as well as to its session name. It is the one command that does - every other command aimed at that toon still does nothing there. LoginAtCharacterSelect is gone, because `all` with a name sent every box on the PC to log in as that toon: use `-LogToonIn <box> <toon>` and name the box.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **No Invis Casting lets the Cast Stack cast when a mob attacks you or your group.** With No Invis Casting ticked, a mob on you or your group now counts as a fight: once a mob near you has you or someone in your group as its target, outside a city, the Cast Stack casts again, so you help instead of watching. Before, it kept waiting until you hit back, even while the mob was already hitting you.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **No Invis Casting works.** The No Invis Casting box now works: out of combat, the Cast Stack does not cast while you are invisible, and says so once. In combat the box does nothing, and anything a command, script or fight file asks for still casts.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Out-of-combat buffs go up as soon as the fight ends.** NonCombatBuff rows on the Cast Stack no longer wait up to two seconds after a kill: they now read the same in-combat state classic reads. No Invis Casting uses that same state, so it starts holding again as soon as the fight is over.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Pick a path again to walk it again.** A path that ended or could not start on Auto-Hunt stayed ended even after picking it again. Picking it in the list now always asks again. A zone still ends the walk, and the next zone does not walk by itself.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Trade and Inventory.Count find an item with a coloured name.** `ogre trade` said an examined item with a coloured name was not in your bags, and Inventory.Count counted it as none. Both find it now.
- **Fixed** · Windows: **A window can always be dragged back, and Reset UI Windows lost its OFF-SCREEN badge.** A window dropped at the far left now keeps the part of its title bar you can drag on the screen; at a UI scale of 1.5 or more only its buttons showed. Resizing, a window getting narrower, a tall InnerSpace console and moving a hidden window can no longer take a title bar off the screen either. So Reset UI Windows drops its OFF-SCREEN badge, and `ogre ui report` says HIDDEN BY CONSOLE for a window the console has hidden.
- **Fixed** · Windows: **Temp Adornments spot a two-handed weapon.** A profile's Primary (two-handed) adornment is now used when your primary weapon is a two-hander, as in the classic bot. Before, it was used whenever your off-hand was empty, so a one-handed weapon with nothing in the off-hand got the two-handed adornment.
- **Fixed** · Navigation: **A path of one point goes there and finishes.** A looping path of one point, or one whose points were all where you stood, restarted over and over. It now goes to the point and finishes, loop or not.
- **Fixed** · Crafting: **Zoning while the craft bot reads your recipe book no longer leaves it short.** If you zoned while the craft bot was reading your recipe book, it could keep a partial list and then say a quest's recipes were not in your book. It now starts the read again after an interruption, and reads the book again if a recipe the game just gave you is missing. It also checks whether the book is really open, so it no longer closes a book you already had open.
- **Fixed** · Other: **A cast monitoring radius set from a script now holds.** `SetCastMonitoring[...,-radius,80]` and `ogre npccast radius` now keep their radius when that same call turns NPC Cast Monitoring on. Before, the console said 80 and the scan ran at 50. The radius now beats the zone's own radius, lasts through Reload and window edits, and ends at the next zone or when the box is unticked.
- **Fixed** · Other: **A fight's cast watch keeps working when you reopen OgreBot.** Reopening OgreBot in the middle of a fight no longer leaves its cast watch doing nothing. If the fight turned NPC Cast Monitoring on and that was not saved, the box comes back on when OgreBot opens. If you turned the box off during the fight, it stays off.
- **Fixed** · Other: **Cast monitoring requests hold before the bot loads and during a zone.** `SetCastMonitoring[...,-watch,"<mob>"]`, `-radius` and `ogre npccast watch` or `radius` given before OgreBot is loaded now tick NPC Cast Monitoring once it loads. Given during a zone change, including from a zone event, they now count for the zone you are arriving in instead of ending when it finishes loading. Unticking the box now always ends a script's mobs and radius.

## 2026.11.06.002 {#r2026-11-06-002}

*Friday, November 6, 2026 at 8:44 PM EST*

- **Changed** · Bot: **A missing or out-of-date ability export now loads OgreBot paused.** OgreBot used to load running on an old export, or on none. It now loads paused, as classic OgreBot did, and says so in the export prompt. It stays paused after the export finishes, so resume it when you are ready.
- **Changed** · Bot: **The AbilityViaDebug box on the Debug tab is now Ability query debug.** On the Debug tab, the box labelled AbilityViaDebug now reads Ability query debug. It is the same box, and your saved setting carries over.
- **Changed** · Bot: **The HO icon lookup reads Classic's -No options.** `OgreBotAPI.Abilities.FindCastableHOIconAbilityID` now reads the -No options Classic's `Get_HOIconAbility` took, such as -NoStun and -NoRoot, on top of your settings boxes. An option it does not use is named once on the console and ignored, where before it was dropped without a word.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Two more ability calls have names that say what they do.** `${OgreBotAPI.Abilities.InterruptID[actor]}` is now `${OgreBotAPI.Abilities.FindCastableInterruptID[actor]}`, and `${OgreBotAPI.Abilities.HOIconAbility[icon,...]}` is now `${OgreBotAPI.Abilities.FindCastableHOIconAbilityID[icon,...]}`. Each one finds an ability you can cast right now and gives back its id, without casting it. The arguments are the same and the old names are gone, so change them in your scripts.
- **Changed** · Bot: **Watcher alerts make their sound, and Track Shiny sees the whole zone.** Find now plays the alarm when a listed name appears or leaves, on the session that saw it, as classic OgreBot did. Track Shiny now dings every session when it finds a new shiny, and it looks through the whole zone, nearest first, where it used to stop at 100 metres.
- **Changed** · Other: **ogre export says where a script's options go.** `ogre export` still takes only -start, -default and stop, and now says where the options go when it refuses a word: a script passes them to `ISXOgreEQ2.Export:Abilities`, for example `ISXOgreEQ2.Export:Abilities[-tradeskill]`. The Classic conversion guide says the same, and it now answers for the old words `ogre tell`, `ogre mapper` and `ogre det`.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **A blank movement key call goes back to your key bind.** `OgreBotAPI.Movement:SetForwardKey`, `SetBackwardKey`, `SetFlyUpKey` and `SetFlyDownKey` with no key now go back to your own key bind; they used to set w, s, Home or End, whatever you had bound. Naming a key works as before, until your key binds next change. A script that named a key before anything had moved no longer leaves the other movement keys unset.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **A misspelt section name is reported in every data file.** Every data file you can layer with your own copy now prints one warning naming a section it does not know, such as "Zones" for "zones", where before the section was skipped without a word. Only that section is skipped, and a name starting with _ is a note. A crafting mats list with no "key" now says it was not used, and a Hide Effects add or remove for a group that does not exist says it did nothing, instead of "skipped".
- **Fixed** · Bot: **A relay group in the list is one name everywhere.** A group in the relay group list is now joined at profile load and left on close under the name you typed. A name with a space or a symbol used to become a squashed copy there, so the toon stayed in the real group after closing. `im trade` now gives the real reason an entry stopped short, such as the partner cancelling, instead of always saying the window cap was reached.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Ability checks know when you are dead or stunned.** Abilities.CanCastOn now answers dead or stunned, and heroic opportunities and the ability query stop picking abilities then, honouring the two stun checkboxes as the cast stack does. The ability query also uses the same range, movement and position checks as everything else, and Lifeburn and Manaburn are no longer refused for the IsReady test the cast stack skips.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Allow Specials to be Targets and Enable PvP now work.** Both boxes now do what they say: a Special actor, or an enemy player, can be your kill target, so the Cast Stack, auto-attack and movement act on it. A few zones whose fights need a Special allow it with the box off, as classic OgreBot did. A shiny now also waits for your cast to finish before the bot goes for it.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Cancelling a cast for a cure potion now drinks the potion.** With X Abilities to Auto Cure Potions ticked, the bot cancels your cast and drinks the potion, even with the auto cure potion box off. Before, it could cancel cast after cast and never drink, and a potion on recast could read as ready. It leaves a cure on the bar alone, stops if the cast stack or its cures are switched off, and does nothing while paused or told not to cure.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **CastInterrupt keeps your cast when no interrupt is ready.** Abilities:CastInterrupt looks for a ready interrupt first and cancels what you are casting only if it found one. Before, it dropped your cast and then found nothing to interrupt with, unlike the classic bot and the fight-file castInterrupt step.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Fighters use a Formless Energy Inverter to dispel.** A fighter holding a Handcrafted Formless Energy Inverter now uses it when a fight calls for a dispel. The bot searched for it in a way known to miss Formless items, so the dispel could stay silent, and a recast on it could read as ready. It now finds all five inverters the way the classic bot did.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Five smaller fixes from the audit.** Consume Items waits for a newly seen item's details, so its timer uses the real duration instead of 10 minutes. Looting forgets what it opened after every zoning, evacs included, and the Codex token button opens the API tokens page. Asking for Confront Fear only counts dirges within 100 m, and Call of the Veteran uses your item unless the porter nearby is yours.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **GetQuest stops on a dialogue it does not know.** GetQuest now clicks through a conversation only when the first reply is the row's opening line. Any other reply stops the run, clicks nothing and prints what it saw, and a first reply that merely contains the finished text now stops as maxed out on faction. A custom row must now have startingQuestText, or its file is refused with a line saying so.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Heroic Opportunities no longer pick stealth abilities.** The Heroic Opportunity picker now skips hostile abilities that require stealth, even while you are stealthed, as the classic bot does. Before, a stealthed scout could spend one on an HO icon.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Long game text no longer reads as empty.** A long quest step, stat tooltip, reply row or script variable now reads whole. Before, anything over about 1,000 characters came back empty.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Looting carries on between mobs.** In combat with nothing to attack, looting now follows Loot Corpses/Chests, as classic OgreBot did, so corpses and chests in reach are taken in the gap between mobs instead of waiting for combat to end. Loot InCombat now rules only while you have something to attack. Shinies still wait until you are out of combat.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Move behind and move in front do not back into a wall.** A toon too close to the mob now only backs out when the way to its spot is clear, as Classic did. Before, it kept backing into a wall or a ledge.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Names and commands with spaces stay whole.** A relay group name, a zone script path or a quoted word in a Load, OnZone or Unload list line, a chat or spawn event's code, or an NPC cast rule's command now stays in one piece instead of splitting at the space. Write such a line as you would type it in the console; a backslash before a quote is no longer needed. An NPC cast rule's OgreBotAPI command now runs once, where it used to run twice.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Names with a backslash or a quote reach the game whole.** Every item, ability, quest, zone and key name the bot puts into a game command is now quoted the same way, so a colour code, a backslash or a double quote in one arrives whole instead of losing a character or breaking the command. Fight files, zone files and travel data are no longer refused for holding a double quote in such a name. `Comms:ExecuteEvent` sends each argument whole again, so one with a comma stays one argument.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Ogre Follow: 0 means the default, and a missing leader stops it.** A distance of 0 for -ogrefollow now means its default instead of being refused. A leader who cannot be found now turns follow off, as Classic did, instead of leaving you following the last one.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Quest Helper holds object clicks after a hail.** Quest Helper no longer clicks a nearby object in the 5 seconds after a hail or an answered reply, as in the classic bot. A dialogue it has no answer for now writes one log line, not one every half second.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Quiver slots, stale research rows, and Allow Camp Spot on move commands.** The quiver refill now moves ammo to the container's next open slot, and Ability Research reports a row missing from your export and starts the next one, 3 seconds after research ends. Announce fills *target* in any case, Tell To Target only tells players, and auto-accepted missions share with the group when Auto Share Missions is on. With Allow Camp Spot off, a character now ignores Come2Me and Move2Area, as classic OgreBot did.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **Sharing a mission follows the classic rules.** -ShareAllMissions and -ShareMission now stop if you leave the zone before the group has gathered, as the zone share already did. -ShareQuest shares straight away with whoever is here, where it used to wait up to 5 minutes for the group. A quest only counts as a mission for -ShareMission if its category is Mission or Mission: Weekly.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **The depot-all option leaves ammo and potions in your bags.** `ogre im depot -depotall` was also emptying your ammo and your poisons, potions and totems into their depots. It now uses the seven depots classic OgreBot used, and `-ada` still deposits all your ammo when you ask for it.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **The misspelt section warning no longer says the file still loads.** The warning for a section name the bot does not know no longer ends with "The file still loads". If the section was the only one the file had, the next line says the file was not used, and the two lines disagreed.
- **Fixed** · Bot: **The performance lists stop showing a diagnostic's name.** A diagnostic's name could show in the performance lists, the lag dump and the log, and now it does not. The Migration Assistant's InnerSpace profile item says Add what is missing, and its text says Classic. A Craft Grandmasters row for a recipe not in your book is no longer green, the Analysis Actors list shows its last count, and the Assign keys hint names gamepad buttons.
