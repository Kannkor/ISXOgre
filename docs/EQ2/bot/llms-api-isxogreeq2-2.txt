== ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer ==

${ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer.Running}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer
  what: TRUE while a house-to-guild-hall, guild-hall-to-house or small-to-large depot transfer is running. Only one transfer runs at a time; starting another is refused with a busy warning.
  returns: bool: TRUE while a depot transfer is running; FALSE when idle.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer.Running}

${ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer.Status}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer
  what: The running transfer's current depot type, its place in the list and how many withdrawals have been made, or "idle". It is the same text `${ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer}` returns.
  returns: string: "idle", or "<type> (n/total, k pulled)" where type is harvest, ammo, adorn, food, lore, potion, collectible, fuel or scroll.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer.Status}

ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer:GHToHouse[flags...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer
  what: The engine behind `ogre ghtohouse`: must start in your guild hall, then withdraws each chosen guild hall depot and deposits it into the matching personal house depot, travelling back and forth until that depot is empty (at most 40 trips per type). If a house depot says it cannot hold any more, the rest goes back into the guild hall depot and that type is skipped; each house depot must be within 8 m of where you arrive in the house.
  arg flags... (string): -all (the eight shared depot types, not scroll), or any of -harvest/-h, -ammo, -adorn, -food, -lore, -potion, -collect/-collectible/-c, -fuel, each with a -no twin (-noharvest/-nh, -nocollect/-nocollectible/-nc, -nofuel ...); read left to right so `-all -nofuel` is all but fuel; a bare word is the house owner to enter (default your own name); an unknown flag is warned about and ignored; at least one depot type must end up selected or the call is refused; -scroll adds the scroll depot, which -all does not include
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer:GHToHouse[-harvest,-scroll]

ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer:HouseToGH[flags...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer
  what: The engine behind `ogre housetogh`: empties the chosen personal house depots into the matching guild hall depots, travelling between the guild hall and the house as often as needed (at most 40 trips per depot type), and pulling Superior Rune items one at a time. Started in the guild hall it first deposits what you carry there; each guild hall depot must be within 11 m of where you arrive, and a missing door or depot stops the run with a console warning.
  arg flags... (string): -all (the eight shared depot types, not scroll), or any of -harvest/-h, -ammo, -adorn, -food, -lore, -potion, -collect/-collectible/-c, -fuel, each with a -no twin (-noharvest/-nh, -nocollect/-nocollectible/-nc, -nofuel ...); read left to right so `-all -nofuel` is all but fuel; a bare word is the house owner to enter (default your own name); an unknown flag is warned about and ignored; at least one depot type must end up selected or the call is refused; -scroll is warned about as unknown here (there is no house scroll depot)
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer:HouseToGH[-all,-nofuel]

ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer:SmallToLarge[flags...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer
  what: The engine behind `ogre smalldepottobigdepot`: in your guild hall, withdraws one depot type from "<type> Depot" and deposits it into "<type> Depot (Large)" (within 11 m), repeating until the small depot is empty. It is refused outside a guild hall or for an unknown type; with the do-not-deposit option it fills your bags once and stops.
  arg flags... (string, default: -harvest): one depot type: -harvest, -adorn/-adornment, -ammo, -scroll/-spell, -collectible/-collectable, -food/-drink, -fuel, -lore/-legend or -potion/-poison/-totem (the last one given wins); TRUE, 1 or -donotdeposit only fills your bags, FALSE or 0 turns that off again; any other word refuses the call
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer:SmallToLarge[-adorn]

ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer:Stop
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer
  what: Stops the running depot transfer and prints "depot transfer: stopped."; does nothing when idle. You stay where the transfer had got to, and items already withdrawn stay in your bags.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.DepotTransfer:Stop


== ISXOgreEQ2.Det ==

${ISXOgreEQ2.Det.DetrimentalInfo[mainIconID,backdropIconID,-actorID,-maxEffects,field]}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Det
  what: One field of a detrimental effect matched by its icon pair: on you (no -actorID, or your own ID) it reads the bot's detrimental tracker, which refreshes about every 500 ms, and for any other actor it walks that actor's effect list live, the first 10 effects unless -maxEffects says otherwise. Numeric fields (exists, id, mainiconid, backdropiconid, currentincrements, duration, maxduration, iseffectinfoavailable) come back as a number - 0 when not found, duration in whole seconds, and always 0 for duration on another actor - while any other field name (Name, Description, Type...) reads the game's effect info, a server request, and the read is NULL out of the world or with fewer than two arguments.
  arg mainIconID (int): the effect's MainIconID
  arg backdropIconID (int): the effect's BackDropIconID
  arg -actorID (switch, default: 0 (you)): -actorID <id>: read this actor's effects instead of yours
  arg -maxEffects (switch, default: 10): -maxEffects <n>: how many of another actor's effects to walk; 0 walks them all; not used for you
  arg field (string, default: exists): the field to return; the last word that is not a switch wins. A read changes nothing: ISXOgreEQ2.Det:DetrimentalCancel is the way to cancel
  returns: int64 or string: exists is 1 or 0; id, icon ids, currentincrements, duration and maxduration are numbers (0 when not found); any other field is a string (empty when not found).
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Det.DetrimentalInfo[176,313,-actorID,${Target.ID},currentincrements]}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Det.DetrimentalInfo_JSON[mainIconID,backdropIconID,-actorID,-maxEffects,-ExtraFields]}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Det
  what: A detrimental effect as JSON (ID, BackDropIconID, MainIconID, CurrentIncrements, Duration, MaxDuration): with no icon pair (or 0,0) a JSON array of every detrimental on you (or of another actor's first effects with -actorID), and with an icon pair that one effect as an object, {} when not found. The switches are read in any position and the first two other arguments are the icon pair; Duration and MaxDuration are always 0 for another actor, and the read is NULL out of the world.
  arg mainIconID (int, default: 0): the effect's MainIconID; 0 with backdropIconID 0 lists every effect
  arg backdropIconID (int, default: 0): the effect's BackDropIconID; with fewer than two icon arguments there is no pair (0,0)
  arg -actorID (switch, default: 0 (you)): -actorID <id>: read this actor's effects instead of yours
  arg -maxEffects (switch, default: 10): -maxEffects <n>: how many of another actor's effects to walk; 0 walks them all; not used for you
  arg -ExtraFields (switch, default: off): -ExtraFields: add Name, Description, Type, UsesRemaining, NumEffectStrings and EffectStrings to a matched effect (a server request); not applied to the whole list
  returns: string: a JSON array (every effect) or a JSON object (one matched effect, {} when not found).
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Det.DetrimentalInfo_JSON}

ISXOgreEQ2.Det:DetrimentalCacheClear
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Det
  what: Empties the bot's detrimental tracker, so its next scan sees every detrimental still on you as new. That fires OgreEvent_OnDetrimentalAdded again for each of them and echoes a +det line for each.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Det:DetrimentalCacheClear

ISXOgreEQ2.Det:DetrimentalCancel[mainIconID,backdropIconID]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Det
  what: Cancels the detrimental effect on you with this icon pair, immediately, and echoes a cancel line to the console when it finds it. It says nothing when no such effect is on you; OgreBotAPI.Cures:CancelDetrimental is the same cancel with a for-who and a not-found message.
  arg mainIconID (int): the effect's MainIconID
  arg backdropIconID (int): the effect's BackDropIconID
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Det:DetrimentalCancel[176,313]

ISXOgreEQ2.Det:Dump[mode,-noinfo]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Det
  what: The `ogre dets` console dump: lists every detrimental on you from the tracker (dets, the default), your maintained spells and their targets (m), or your target's effects (target), one line each. By default it also reads each detrimental's Name and Description, a server request per effect, which -noinfo skips; out of the world it only prints a warning.
  arg mode (string, default: dets): dets (or det, d), m (or maintained), target (or t); -h, -help or ? as the first word prints usage
  arg -noinfo (switch, default: off): skip the Name and Description reads; -info turns them back on
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Det:Dump[dets,-noinfo]

ISXOgreEQ2.Det:Set_DetrimentalScanTime[ms]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Det
  what: Sets how often, in milliseconds, the bot's detrimental tracker rescans your detrimentals (500 by default); every reader of the tracker sees the change. A value outside 100 to 5000 is refused with a console warning and the interval stays as it was, and the setting is not saved.
  arg ms (int): the scan interval in milliseconds, 100 to 5000
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Det:Set_DetrimentalScanTime[250]

== ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter ==

${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.ActiveCount}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: The number of encounters that are setting up or running right now.
  returns: int: encounters in setup or active; 0 when none.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.ActiveCount}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.Current}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: The encounters setting up or running right now, as a JSON array. Each entry has primary (the first named mob), state ("setup" or "active"), named (every named mob), expansion and zone.
  returns: string: a JSON array of the active encounters; "[]" when none.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.Current}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.IsActive[mob]}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Whether any loaded encounter naming that mob is setting up or running. The mob name is matched ignoring case, whatever zone you are in.
  arg mob (string): The named mob's name.
  returns: bool: TRUE when that mob's encounter is in setup or active; FALSE when it is not, when no encounter owns that name, or when no name is given.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.IsActive[${Target.Name}]}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.Loaded}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: The number of encounter files loaded, across every zone. It changes when a reload or a zone-in picks up new or edited files.
  returns: int: the number of loaded encounters.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.Loaded}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.Sequences}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Every live encounter sequence as a JSON array, so you can see which step a fight is waiting on. Each entry names its encounter and holds either its condition rules or the list it is running (setup, a trigger and so on) with the current step.
  returns: string: a JSON array of live sequences; "[]" when none.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.Sequences}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.Validation}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: The answer of the last Encounter:Validate, as JSON: ok (true when the file would load), errors and warnings (how many), fileErrors and ruleErrors (the errors split: a file error stops the file from loading, a rule error does not), and issues - one for each line it printed, with the list, the rule and the step it belongs to (counted from 1), the message, and whether it is only a warning. Empty until Encounter:Validate has run.
  returns: string: JSON, for example {"ok":true,"errors":0,"warnings":0,"fileErrors":0,"ruleErrors":0,"issues":[]}; "" before the first Encounter:Validate.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter.Validation}
  more: https://kannkor.github.io/ISXOgre/EQ2/bot/developer/zone-files/builder

ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Debug[switch,value]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Turns the console echo of encounter messages (comms) or step changes (steps) on or off, then prints both settings; with no switch it only prints them - the same as `ogre encounter debug`. Both are always written to the log; an unknown switch or a missing on/off prints a warning and changes nothing.
  arg switch (string, default: ""): comms or steps; blank just prints the current settings.
  arg value (string, default: ""): on or off; needed when switch is given.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Debug[steps,on]

ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Fire[mob]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Sets up for an encounter on the next pulse: the fight of the named mob given, or with no name the nearest or engaged named in this zone; a setup already running is started again. It is OgreBotAPI.Bot:SetUpFor without the for-who, and it is ignored with a warning when Enable Raid Setups (raid zones) or Enable Heroic Setups (other zones) is off, or when no encounter owns that name.
  arg mob (string, default: ""): The named mob whose encounter to set up; blank picks the nearest or engaged named in this zone.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Fire[${Target.Name}]

ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Help
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Prints the `ogre encounter` words (reload, debug and sequences) with one line each to the console, the same as typing a bare `ogre encounter`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Help

ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:List
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Prints this zone's encounter coding to the console: one line per fight with its named mobs, (DISABLED) when switched off, and its file. It always answers, even "No encounter coding for this zone", and is OgreBotAPI.Encounter:List without the for-who.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:List

ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Reload
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Re-reads every encounter file from disk in the background and swaps the new set in when it is done, which ends every encounter setup and sequence in progress - the same as `ogre encounter reload`. It prints one line at once and a second with the loaded, scanned and error counts when the swap lands.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Reload

ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Sequences
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Prints the Sequences JSON to the console, or "(none live)" when nothing is running - the same as `ogre encounter sequences`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Sequences

ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Validate[root,path]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter
  what: Checks one fight file by the same rules the game uses when it loads fight files, and changes nothing: a file on disk by its root and path (the same as `ogre encounter validate`), or the base64 text of a fight file's JSON. It prints whether the file would load and each problem, and keeps the answer for Encounter.Validation.
  arg root (string): The root the fight file is in: default or custom. With no path, this one argument is read as the base64 text of a fight file's JSON, checked as a new file of your own.
  arg path (string, default: none): The fight file's path inside that root, ending in .json. A path with spaces may be quoted or written as several words.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Encounter:Validate[custom,"My Zone/My Mob.json"]
  more: https://kannkor.github.io/ISXOgre/EQ2/bot/developer/zone-files/builder


== ISXOgreEQ2.Events ==

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Count}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: How many native game events the extension listens to, currently 14, numbered from 0 for the event argument of Received, Dispatched, Subscribers, EventName and NativeName.
  returns: int: the number of events (14 today)
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Count}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Dispatched[<event>]}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: How many arrivals of this game event have been handed out to the extension's features since it loaded, or the total for all events when the argument is left out. It trails Received only by what is still Pending, and an unknown event gives NULL.
  arg <event> (string): The event's 0-based number or its name, such as ActorSpawned; leave it out for the total across all events
  returns: int64: arrivals handed out for that event since load (or for all events); NULL for an unknown event
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Dispatched[ActorSpawned]}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Dropped}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: Total game events thrown away since the extension loaded, because more than 4096 were waiting at once (oldest dropped first) or the event was not recognised. It never resets.
  returns: int64: events dropped since load
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Dropped}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.EventName[<event>]}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: The extension's name for a game event, such as IncomingChatText, looked up by its 0-based number or its name. A missing or unknown event gives NULL.
  arg <event> (string): The event's 0-based number (0 to Count minus 1) or its name
  returns: string: the event's name; NULL for a missing or unknown event
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.EventName[0]}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.NativeName[<event>]}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: The ISXEQ2 event a game event is read from, such as EQ2_onIncomingChatText, which is also the name a script attaches its own atom to. A missing or unknown event gives NULL.
  arg <event> (string): The event's 0-based number (0 to Count minus 1) or its name, such as IncomingChatText
  returns: string: the ISXEQ2 event name; NULL for a missing or unknown event
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.NativeName[IncomingChatText]}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Pending}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: How many received game events are waiting to be handed to the extension's features; they are handed out every pulse, so this is normally 0. At most 4096 can wait, and beyond that the oldest are dropped and counted in Dropped.
  returns: int: events waiting to be handed out right now
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Pending}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Received[<event>]}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: How many times this game event has arrived since the extension loaded, or the total for all events when the argument is left out. The event is its 0-based number or its name in any case (IncomingChatText, ActorSpawned and so on), and an unknown event gives NULL.
  arg <event> (string): The event's 0-based number or its name, such as IncomingChatText; leave it out for the total across all events
  returns: int64: arrivals of that event since load (or of all events); NULL for an unknown event
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Received[IncomingChatText]}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Stats}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: One summary line, such as attached=yes events=14 pending=0 dropped=0 recv=5120 disp=5120, with recv and disp totalled across all events. Echoing ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events} prints the same line after the word Events.
  returns: string: the summary line
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Stats}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.StatsJSON}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: The full event table as one JSON object: attached, count, pending, dropped, and an events array giving each event's i, label, native, received, dispatched and subscribers.
  returns: string: a JSON object
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.StatsJSON}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Subscribers[<event>]}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Events
  what: How many of the extension's own features are listening to this game event (such as the chat and spawn handling behind OgreBotAPI.Events), or the total when the argument is left out. Atoms a script attaches to the ISXEQ2 event itself are not counted, and an unknown event gives NULL.
  arg <event> (string): The event's 0-based number or its name, such as FinishedZoning; leave it out for the total across all events
  returns: int: listeners on that event (or on all events); NULL for an unknown event
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Events.Subscribers[FinishedZoning]}


== ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment ==

ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment:Boost[flag,count,...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment
  what: Starts boosting the item already selected on the tradeskill window's Experiment tab, picking each queued stat and clicking Begin and the confirm dialog for every boost; it also turns on the crafting reaction handling. It refuses with a console line when you are not in the world, lack the Experiment AA, a run is already going or no stat is queued, and words it does not know are echoed and skipped.
  arg flag (string): the stat to boost: -p, -pot or -potency for potency, -cb or -critbonus for crit bonus, or -<stat> for any other stat label the game offers
  arg count (int): how many boosts of that stat to queue
  arg ... (string): more flag, count pairs, run in the order given
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment:Boost[-p,3,-cb,2]

ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment:Items
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment
  what: Lists the numbered item rows on the Experiment tab's item page to the console, briefly enlarging the tradeskill window to read them and then putting it back. It prints a warning instead when you are not in the world, lack the Experiment AA, a run is in progress or no list is showing.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment:Items

ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment:Run[row,flag,count,...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Experiment

  what: The run's verdict: idle (nothing has run), pending (a run is in flight), completed, completedWithFailures (a steps zone ended but a step did not do its job) or failed. A skipped zone and a stopped zone script run read completed, while a stopped steps zone or a stopped zone-in reads failed.
  returns: string: "idle", "pending", "completed", "completedWithFailures" or "failed".
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.IC.Verdict}

${ISXOgreEQ2.IC.Zone}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: The full zone name (with its difficulty) of the current or last run. It is set when a zone file starts, so while IC is still zoning in it names the last zone.
  returns: string: the run's full zone name; "" before any zone has started.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.IC.Zone}

${ISXOgreEQ2.IC.ZoneEnd}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Where a script zone's zone end stands after it called IC:ZoneEnd: none (not asked), busy (calling the group to the guild hall or staying in the zone), halted (halt at end of zone, waiting for Resume), zoneOut (zone out now), calledGH (the group is in the guild hall, so do not zone out) or ghFailed (the call failed; end the zone as usual). It goes back to none when a new run starts or the run is stopped.
  returns: string: "none", "busy", "halted", "zoneOut", "calledGH" or "ghFailed".
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.IC.ZoneEnd}

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Check[zone]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Prints which zone files claim a zone (exact or prefix match) and whether the zone is available according to the lockout table; no zone means the one you are in. When the table is out of date it asks for a fresh read and says to ask again shortly, except while IC is paused, you are out of the world or a read is already pending, when it only says the table is not fresh.
  arg zone (string): the zone name to check; leave it off for your current zone
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Check["${Zone.Name}"]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Complete[note]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: For a zone run by a script: marks the run completed, so it is scored COMPLETED when the script exits, while a script that exits without calling this is scored FAILED. It does not end the run, prints nothing, does nothing when no run is in flight, and a steps zone, which completes itself, refuses it with one line.
  arg note (string): optional text kept as the run's note; empty gives "reported complete"
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Complete["all nameds killed"]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:End
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Closes IC out, the same as the IC window's X and `ogre end ic`: the window closes, IC's pause clears, and a running zone and list stop as with Stop. Unlike Stop it does not cancel end-of-list actions already under way and says nothing when nothing is running.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:End

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Fail[reason]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Ends the current run at once as FAILED with the reason, printed on the console; a zone script keeps running, a steps zone stops at its current step without its exit steps, and a zone-in stops its route. Does nothing when no run is in flight.
  arg reason (string): why it failed; empty gives "the script reported failure", or on a steps zone "IC:Fail was called with no reason"
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Fail["group member missing"]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:List[filter]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Prints every loaded zone file (folder, path, zone, difficulties, and [delegates] for one that hands off to another file or a script) and a count. A filter keeps only files whose zone or path contains it, any case, with its words joined by a space.
  arg filter (string): text to match in the zone name or file path; leave it off to list all
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:List[Zobboz]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Open
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Opens the IC window, the same as `ogre ic`. Already open does nothing, and it warns when the UI host is not available.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Open

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Options[switches...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Sets IC options by switch: -<option> turns a tick-box on and -<option>=off turns it off (on, off, true, false, 1, 0, yes or no, any case), while -afterWipe=, -maxWait=, -fileArgs= and -stopAfterZone take values. No argument prints every option and its state; changes are saved and reported in one line, and switches that could not be set are listed.
  arg switches... (string): one or more option switches, each starting with -
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Options[-loop,-skipShinies=off,-maxWait=5400]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Pause
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Turns on IC's own pause, which stops IC moving the zone and the list along while OgreBot, and any fight already under way, keep going. Prints "IC PAUSED" when it changes, and time paused does not count against the max wait.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Pause

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Queue[action,args...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Edits the IC list: add,<tier>,<path>, add,<tier>,<zone name> or add,<zone name> adds a zone file, startat,<row>,<N> makes that row start at step N (N of 1 or less clears it), and clear empties the list. No action, or any other word, prints the list, and add or startat without their arguments is refused.
  arg action (string): add, startat or clear; anything else or nothing prints the list
  arg args... (string): the action's arguments: a tier and path or a zone name for add, a row and step number for startat
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Queue[add,default,"Rage of Cthurath/_04_Zon_Zobboz_The_Graftwerk.json"]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Refresh
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Marks IC's cached zone lockout table out of date, so the next check reads it from the game again. Prints "lock table invalidated - it re-seeds on the next check".
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Refresh

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Reload
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Re-reads every zone file from IC's folders and prints "N zone(s) from M file(s) - X skipped, Y warning(s)". Same as the IC window's Reload and `ogre ic reload`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Reload

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Resume
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Clears IC's own pause and prints "IC resumed" when it changes. It is also how you go on after a halt at end of zone or a pause for a no-trade shiny.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Resume

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Run[tier,relPath]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Starts one zone file (not the list): when you are not in its zone and the travel data knows the way, IC first takes the group there, then a zone that hands off to a script launches it with no arguments (the script reads ${ISXOgreEQ2.IC.Value}) and a steps zone runs its own steps. It refuses with a console line when you are not in the world, a run is already in flight, IC is paused, OgreBot has no profile loaded, or the file is missing, not valid or disabled.
  arg tier (string): which zone file folder: default (the shipped files) or custom (your own, under OgreSave/EQ2/IC/custom)
  arg relPath (string): the zone file's path inside that folder; further arguments are joined to it with a space
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Run[default,"Rage of Cthurath/_04_Zon_Zobboz_The_Graftwerk.json"]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:RunList[row]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Starts the queued list from a row (1-based, default the top); a row the queue does not have, or one that is not a number, prints "no row N (the queue has M)" and starts nothing. It refuses with a console line when you are not in the world, a run or the end-of-list actions are still going, or the queue is empty.
  arg row (int, default: 1): the list row to start at, counting from 1
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:RunList[2]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:SetFileArgs[text]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Sets the IC File Args text and saves it; no argument clears it. Only the first argument is used, so quote text that holds commas.
  arg text (string): the new IC File Args text; empty clears it
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:SetFileArgs["fast loot"]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:SetFileOptionLabel[n,text]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Sets the text the IC window shows for IC File Option n (1-3); empty text goes back to the zone file's own label or "IC File Option n". The label is cleared when the next zone starts, and n outside 1-3 prints a warning.
  arg n (int): which IC File Option, 1 to 3
  arg text (string): the label to show; empty restores the default
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:SetFileOptionLabel[1,"Kill the optional named"]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:SetMaxWait[seconds]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Sets Max wait per zone in seconds and saves it; 0 waits forever. A fraction rounds up, a number below 0 becomes 1 and a huge one is held at the largest value, while text that is not a number (or no argument) changes nothing, each with a console line.
  arg seconds (number): seconds a zone may run; 0 means no limit
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:SetMaxWait[5400]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Status
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Prints IC's report on the console: zone files loaded and their folders, both pauses, the lockout table, the run, its verdict and max wait, the current step, the list and the options that are on. Same as `ogre ic status`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Status

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Stop
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Stops the run and the list where they stand and clears IC's pause: a zone script is no longer tracked but is NOT ended, and a steps zone stops at its current step without running its exit steps. With nothing running it cancels end-of-list actions still under way, or prints "nothing is running" (same as the IC window's End and `ogre ic stop`).
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Stop

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Validate[tier,path]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Checks one zone file by the same rules the Instance Controller uses when it loads zone files, and changes nothing: a file on disk by its tier and path (the same as `ogre ic validate`), or the base64 text of a zone file's JSON. It prints whether the file would load and each problem, and keeps the answer for IC.Validation.
  arg tier (string): The root the zone file is in: default or custom. With no path, this one argument is read as the base64 text of a zone file's JSON.
  arg path (string, default: none): The zone file's path inside that root, ending in .json.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Validate[custom,"My_Zone.json"]
  more: https://kannkor.github.io/ISXOgre/EQ2/bot/developer/zone-files/builder

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Value[key]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Prints the run's values on the console: no key prints the whole document and the path of its saved copy, a dotted key prints one value, "" (empty) for empty text, or "NULL (absent)". Before any run it prints "no values - nothing has run yet"; a script reads values with ${ISXOgreEQ2.IC.Value[key]} instead.
  arg key (string): a dotted key such as zone.fullName; leave it off to print the whole document
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Value[zone.fullName]

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Wipe
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Declares a wipe now: a steps zone that is running its enter or steps list stops and runs its wipe recovery. Out of the world, or with no such zone running, it prints why and does nothing.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:Wipe

ISXOgreEQ2.IC:ZoneEnd
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.IC
  what: Called by a zone script where it would zone out: IC runs the zone end a steps zone gets (halt at end of zone, then Call to GH after each zone or Stay in zone until a zone is ready, as ticked), and the script then reads ${ISXOgreEQ2.IC.ZoneEnd} until it is not busy or halted. With no zone script running, or on a steps zone, it prints a warning and does nothing.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.IC:ZoneEnd


== ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps ==

${ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps.Busy}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps
  what: TRUE while an inventory flow started through this object (Overflow, MoveBag or Trade) is running. Only one of the three runs at a time, and starting another while one runs is refused with a busy warning.
  returns: bool: TRUE while an overflow, bag move or trade flow is running; FALSE when idle.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps.Busy}

${ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps.Status}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps
  what: One line naming the running inventory flow and its progress, or "idle". It is the same text `${ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps}` returns.
  returns: string: "idle", "overflow (N popped)", "mbc (done/total)" or "trade (done/total)".
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps.Status}

ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps:MoveBag[from,to]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps
  what: The engine behind `ogre mbc`: moves every item in carried bag `from` into bag `to`, one item every 0.2 s, where a bag's number is its inventory slot (1-6). It is refused with a console warning when a number is missing or out of range, both are the same bag, a slot holds no bag, the source bag is empty, or another inventory flow is running.
  arg from (int): bag number (1-6) to empty
  arg to (int): bag number (1-6) to move the items into
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps:MoveBag[2,3]

ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps:Overflow
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps
  what: The engine behind `ogre overflow`: pops items out of the overflow window into your bags, one every 0.2 s, until the game says the overflow is empty or has no room, only one bag slot is left free, or 300 items have been popped. It returns at once and the work runs on later frames; it is refused with a console warning when another inventory flow is running or you are not in the world.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps:Overflow

ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps:Stop
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps
  what: Drops whichever overflow, bag move or trade flow is running, without printing anything; does nothing when idle. An open trade window is left as it is.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps:Stop

ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps:Trade[count]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps
  what: The engine behind `ogre trade`: opens a trade with your current target, adds up to `count` whole stacks from your bags of the item you have EXAMINED (exact name, any case), then accepts. You need a target and an open examine window; when nothing is examined or no stack matches, the run rejects the trade it opened and ends with a console line.
  arg count (int, default: 12): most stacks to hand over; missing, not a whole number, or 0 or less uses 12
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.InvOps:Trade[4]

== ISXOgreEQ2.MCP ==

${ISXOgreEQ2.MCP.ForWho}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: The for-who text MCP buttons and bound keys use on this session, from the For Who picker or MCP:SetForWho. A button with its own for-who still uses its own.
  returns: string: the for-who text in force on this session; all when nothing has been picked (everyone).

${ISXOgreEQ2.MCP.IrcConnected}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Whether this session last heard that the IRC link is up. It starts FALSE on a new session and stays FALSE until the IRC hub reports in; while FALSE, MCP buttons that send over IRC go through OgreConsole instead.
  returns: bool: TRUE when the IRC hub last reported the link connected; FALSE when it reported it down or has not reported yet.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.MCP.IrcConnected}

  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP

The bind is saved to Scripts/OgreSave/EQ2/OgreMCPBinds.json, armed at once, and every EQ2 session on this PC picks it up; a bind with the same name is replaced, and a button id that has no command yet only prints a note.
  arg key (string): The InnerSpace key name to bind, such as G600-G9 or F13.
  arg buttonId (string): The MCP button to run, as MCP:Buttons lists it.
  arg name (string, default: MCP_<key>_<buttonId> with every other character turned into _): A name for the bind, used by MCP:RemoveBind.
  arg event (string, default: press): release fires when the key is let go; anything else fires on press.

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Buttons
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Prints every configured MCP button to the console with its id, label, page and command, so you can find the id to give MCP:Fire or MCP:AddBind. With no saved layout it prints a line saying there are no buttons yet.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Buttons

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Close
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Closes the Master Control Panel together with its For Who, Build command and Assign keys windows. It also cancels a key test that is still waiting for a press.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Close

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Fire[buttonId]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Runs an MCP button by its id, exactly as clicking it would: the button's command is read from the saved layout at that moment and sent with the button's own for-who, or this session's For Who when the button has none. A missing id, no saved layout, or a button with no command prints one console line and sends nothing.
  arg buttonId (string): The button's id as MCP:Buttons lists it: btn:<id> (follows the button when it moves), static:<row>_<col> or page:<page>:<row>_<col>.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Fire[static:0_0]

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:FixHoistOrder
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Repairs buttons that an older MCP:Migrate wrote with some arguments in the wrong order, touching only the affected buttons and writing OgreMCP.json.bak first. It runs once per file (a second call prints that it is already done), prints how many buttons were fixed or need checking by hand, and has every EQ2 session on this PC reload when anything changed.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:FixHoistOrder

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:ListBinds
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Prints every saved key bind to the console: its name, key, press or release, the button it runs, and whether it is disabled.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:ListBinds

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Migrate[overwrite]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Converts the old OgreBot MCP buttons (Scripts/EQ2OgreBot/MCP/MCPCustomSettings.xml) into the new panel's Scripts/OgreSave/EQ2/OgreMCP.json and prints a summary; button types with no command equivalent come across as label only. It refuses to replace an existing OgreMCP.json unless you pass overwrite, and on success every EQ2 session on this PC reloads the panel.
  arg overwrite (string, default: off - an existing file is kept): The word overwrite (or 1, TRUE, yes, on) replaces an existing OgreMCP.json, discarding its current layout.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Migrate

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Open
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Opens the Master Control Panel (MCP), the window of buttons that send commands to your characters, with its saved layout; the same as `ogre mcp`, and alt+backtick toggles it. Calling it while the panel is already open does nothing; if the in-game UI host is not running or a panel file cannot be read, nothing opens and one console line says which, and a missing command catalog opens the builder empty with a line.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Open

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Reload
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Re-reads Scripts/OgreSave/EQ2/OgreMCP.json and updates an open panel to match, which is how a change saved on one session shows up on the others. It is skipped when the file is exactly what this session just saved, or when the file is not valid JSON.

  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Re-reads Scripts/OgreSave/EQ2/OgreMCPBinds.json, removes this session's live key binds and arms the saved ones again. Adding or removing a bind on any session runs this on every EQ2 session on the PC.

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:RemoveBind[name]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Removes a saved key bind by name, disarms it and tells every EQ2 session on this PC to drop it. An unknown name prints a console line and changes nothing.
  arg name (string): The bind's name as MCP:ListBinds shows it.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:RemoveBind[G9Heal]

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Send[message,-tab,tab,-rg,group,-cfw,who]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: With arguments it sends message exactly as ISXOgreX.Console:Send does (a leading !c or !ci makes it a command, anything else is a chat line). With no arguments it sends the button just clicked on this session, once, through the same route a click uses, and otherwise the console input box line.
  arg message (string, default: the button just clicked, or the input box line): The literal line to send; a leading !c or !ci makes it a command. Leave out every argument for the button just clicked, or the console input box line.
  arg -tab (switch, default: (none)): Also show the line in the console tab named by the next argument; may be given more than once.
  arg tab (string, default: (none)): The console tab name that follows -tab.
  arg -rg (switch, default: (none)): Send to the relay group named by the next argument instead of the bot's own relay group.
  arg group (string, default: the bot's relay group): The relay group name that follows -rg.
  arg -cfw (switch, default: (none)): Give the command a for-who, used by its commands whose own for-who is left as auto.
  arg who (for-who, default: everyone): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names - the value that follows -cfw.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Send["!c -pause","-cfw","igw:${Me.Name}"]

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:SetForWho[value]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Sets the For Who that MCP buttons and bound keys use on this session only, held in memory (not saved, not shared, back to everyone after a restart). With a value it sets that as a custom For Who and shows it in the picker (spaces are removed; all or an empty value clears it back to everyone); with no value it takes the choice the For Who picker just made.
  arg value (string, default: (none) - the picker's choice): The for-who text to use, such as igw:<name> or several names joined with |. Leave it out to take the For Who picker's current choice.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:SetForWho[igw:${Me.Name}]

ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Toggle
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MCP
  what: Opens the Master Control Panel when it is closed and closes it when it is open. This is what alt+backtick runs.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MCP:Toggle


== ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft ==

ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:Help
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft
  what: Prints the `ogre mc` usage lines to the console and does nothing else. It is what `ogre mc help` runs.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:Help

ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:List
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft
  what: Reads the recipes your TARGETED merchant sells and prints them numbered, for use with Start["#n"]. It is refused with a console warning when a run is already going, you are not in the world, the target is not a merchant within 9 m, or you lack the Summon Crafting Stations ability.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:List

ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:Start[words...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft
  what: The engine behind `ogre mc <recipe> [count]`: for each round it buys the recipe from your targeted merchant (only if you do not know it), scribes it, summons the crafting station it needs, empties the craft engine's setup queue and crafts it 3 times. It has the same refusals as List and also refuses with no recipe or while a craft run is going; on a recipe not yet cached it MAKES A SERVER CALL (`.ToRecipeInfo`, to learn the crafting device), and only when the craft panel's "Allow recipe-info server call" option is on, which it is by default.
  arg words... (string): a partial recipe name (one or more words, first match wins) or "#n" for row n of List's numbering, then an optional trailing whole number = rounds (1 to 999, default 1; each round is 3 crafts)
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:Start[ferrite,bracelet,4]

ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:Status
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft
  what: Prints the merchant-craft state (running or idle, the current recipe and round, how many recipes the last scan found) and then the usage lines to the console. It is what bare `ogre mc` and `ogre mc status` run.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:Status

ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:Stop
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft
  what: Abandons the merchant-craft run and prints "mc: stopped.", or "mc: nothing is running." when idle. It does not stop a craft queue the run already started, and a summoned station stays.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantCraft:Stop


== ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps ==

${ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps.Running}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps
  what: TRUE while a buyfuel or buycrate run is in progress. Only one runs at a time; starting another is refused with a busy warning.
  returns: bool: TRUE while a fuel or crate purchase run is going; FALSE when idle.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps.Running}

${ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps.Status}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps
  what: One line naming the running merchant run and its progress, or "idle". It is the same text `${ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps}` returns.
  returns: string: "idle", "buyfuel '<fuel>' (round n)" or "buycrate (done/total)".
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps.Status}

ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps:BuyCrate[flags...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps
  what: The engine behind `ogre buycrate`: targets and opens this zone's crate merchant (who must be nearby), buys one of each chosen heroic and raid crate, accepts the purchase prompt and closes the merchant. Only exp17_dun_fordel_midst and exp18_rgn_svarni_expanse have one (Svarni sells no heroic crate), and anywhere else, or when nothing matches the flags, the call is refused with a warning.
  arg flags... (string, default: -all): -r/-raid for the raid crate, -h/-heroic for the heroic crate, -all for both; read left to right, so `-raid -all` buys both and `-all -raid` only the raid crate; unknown words are ignored
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps:BuyCrate[-h]

ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps:BuyFuel[amount]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps
  what: The engine behind `ogre buyfuel`: at the guild hall Fuel Depot (within 11 m) it counts how much of the fuel you have EXAMINED the depot holds, buys the shortfall from the merchant titled Guild Fuel Merchant and deposits it, repeating for at most 10 rounds. It is refused when nothing is examined, a run is already going or you are not in the world, and stops with a console warning when the depot is missing, too far or does not open.
  arg amount (int, default: 90000): how much of the fuel the depot should end up holding; missing, not a whole number, or 0 or less uses 90000
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps:BuyFuel[20000]

ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps:Stop
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps
  what: Stops a running buyfuel or buycrate and prints "merchant: stopped."; does nothing when idle. Open depot or merchant windows are left as they are.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.MerchantOps:Stop


== ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate ==

ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:Cancel[task]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate
  what: Backs out of a pending overwrite confirmation for one task, putting its row back to its normal state. It does nothing when that task is not waiting for a confirmation or the name is unknown.
  arg task (string): the task whose confirmation to cancel: accounts, imsettings, imlists, profiles, irc, mcp or isprofile
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:Cancel[profiles]

ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:Close
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate
  what: Closes the Migration Assistant window and cancels its pending auto-close. Nothing that has already run is undone.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:Close

ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:Open
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate
  what: Opens the Migration Assistant window (the same as `ogre migrate`), listing each migration task with its status and a re-run button. Before it opens it silently runs any task whose old file exists and whose new file does not, and when the window is already up it does nothing.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:Open

ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:RequestRerun[task]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate
  what: Puts one task's row in the Migration Assistant into its overwrite confirmation state, the same as clicking its re-run button; it reads and writes no files. It only does so when that task's old source data exists, otherwise nothing changes.

  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:RequestRerun[accounts]

ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:Run[task,overwrite]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate
  what: Runs one migration task now, copying old OgreBot data into the new store (your old XML files are kept); the isprofile task instead asks InnerSpace to add its game profile and ignores overwrite. Without overwrite a task whose new file already exists is not rewritten and its row asks you to confirm instead, an unknown task name does nothing, and the result shows in the Migration Assistant window and the log.
  arg task (string): the task to run: accounts, imsettings, imlists, profiles, irc, mcp or isprofile
  arg overwrite (string): the word overwrite (any case) to replace data that already exists; anything else, or leaving it out, keeps existing data
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Migrate:Run[irc]


== ISXOgreEQ2.Nav ==

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavActive}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: TRUE while a nav trip is in progress - planning its route, waiting for this zone's navmesh, or moving. A paused trip still counts as in progress.
  returns: bool: TRUE while a trip is planning, waiting for its navmesh or moving; FALSE when idle or once the last trip has ended (Arrived, Stopped, Failed).
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavActive}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavDistance}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Straight-line distance from the character to the trip's destination, refreshed every pulse while it moves, on foot or flying. It is -1 when idle, while a trip is still planning or waiting for its navmesh, and after a trip ends.
  returns: float: distance in game units to the destination; -1 when no trip is moving.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavDistance}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavMeshOn}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: TRUE unless nav has been switched off with Nav:Set[mesh,off] or `ogre nav -off`. While it is off, new trips are refused; the switch is not saved and starts TRUE each time the extension loads.
  returns: bool: TRUE when nav is allowed to move this character; FALSE when it is switched off.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavMeshOn}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavMeshPolys}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: How many walkable polygons the loaded navmesh has. It is 0 when no mesh is loaded in this zone yet.
  returns: int: polygon count of the loaded mesh; 0 when none is loaded.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavMeshPolys}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavMeshTiles}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: How many tiles the loaded navmesh has. It is 0 when no mesh is loaded in this zone yet.
  returns: int: tile count of the loaded mesh; 0 when none is loaded.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavMeshTiles}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavMeshZone}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Name of the navmesh that is loaded - the zone's short name in lower case, or the base zone's name when this zone is a copy that uses it. It is "" until a mesh is loaded in this zone (see NavOnMap).
  returns: string: the loaded mesh's zone name; "" when none is loaded.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavMeshZone}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavOnMap}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: TRUE when a navmesh is loaded right now, so nav can plan a route without waiting. The mesh loads on the first trip or route request in a zone and is set aside on every zone change, so this reads FALSE after zoning until something asks for a route.
  returns: bool: TRUE when a navmesh is loaded; FALSE when none is loaded yet in this zone.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavOnMap}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavOnMesh}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: TRUE while the current trip is driven over a navmesh route. It is FALSE when idle, while a trip still waits for its navmesh or plans its first route, and once a trip ends.
  returns: bool: TRUE while a trip is driven over a navmesh route; FALSE otherwise.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavOnMesh}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavReason}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Plain words about the trip: while it plans or moves, what the plan is doing; once it has ended or while it waits for a navmesh, why. It is empty before the first trip, and reads "already there" when a trip was asked for the spot the character already stands on.
  returns: string: the plan in force, or why the last trip ended; "" before any trip.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavReason}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavState}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: The trip's state as one word; Arrived, Stopped and Failed stay until the next trip starts, and a paused trip still reads Moving. Stopped means somebody ended the trip on purpose, Failed means nav gave up - NavReason says why.
  returns: string: a state word such as Idle, Planning, WaitingForMesh, Moving, Arrived, Stopped or Failed.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavState}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavWaypoint}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: The waypoint on the current route the character is heading for, counted from 1 (the first point after the start) up to NavWaypoints on the last leg. It is 0 when no route is being walked, a finished trip included.
  returns: int: the waypoint being walked to, 1 to NavWaypoints; 0 when no route is being walked.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavWaypoint}

${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavWaypoints}
  kind: member of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: How many waypoints the current route has after its start point, so NavWaypoint reaches it on the last leg. It is 0 when no route is being walked, a finished trip included.
  returns: int: number of waypoints after the start; 0 when no route is being walked.
  example: echo ${ISXOgreEQ2.Nav.NavWaypoints}

ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:End
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Ends the trip under way and closes the nav window, the same as `ogre end nav`, `ogre nav end` or the window's X. It prints "nav: stopped (...)" only when a trip was under way.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:End

ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Flags[flag...]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Runs the `ogre nav -<flag> ...` line: -loc x,y,z (or -to) starts a trip and applies -precision, -nodoors/-doors and -reset with it (it ignores -set, -on/-off and -panel/-nopanel on the same line), while a line without -loc takes -set key value, -reset [key], -on/-off, -panel/-nopanel, -stop, -status or -help. With no flag it prints a warning and a bad or unknown flag prints an error naming it; -precision and -nodoors stay in force after the trip.
  arg flag... (string): The flags and their values, one per argument; a -loc point may be one "x,y,z" argument or three.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Flags[-loc,${Me.X},${Me.Y},${Math.Calc[${Me.Z}+30]},-precision,3]

ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Help
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Prints the `ogre nav` command help to the console, one line at a time. This is bare `ogre nav` or `ogre nav help`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Help

ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Open
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Opens the nav panel (trip state with Stop and Pause buttons) now; if the window cannot open it prints "nav panel: <reason>". This is `ogre nav panel`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Open

ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Set[key,value]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Changes one nav setting for this session (not saved) and prints "nav: <key> = <value>"; an unknown key, or a value the setting refuses, prints an error and changes nothing. The key is case-insensitive, and `mesh` (alias `on`) never refuses: a yes word switches nav on and any other value switches it off.
  arg key (string): The setting's name, such as arrive, finalarrive, doors or avoid.
  arg value (string): The new value - a number for distances and counts, on or off for switches.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Set[arrive,4]

ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Status
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Prints what nav is doing to the console - state, reason, paused, waypoint progress, the loaded mesh and every setting changed from its default. This is `ogre nav status`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Status

ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Stop
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Ends the trip under way (NavState becomes Stopped) and prints "nav: stopped"; the nav panel stays open. With no trip under way it changes nothing but still prints the line - this is `ogre nav stop`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:Stop

ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:To[x,y,z]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.Nav
  what: Starts a trip that moves the character to the point, replacing any trip already under way; it returns at once and the route is planned over the next frames (watch NavState). A missing value, or one that is not a number, prints an error and starts nothing, and a refusal (not in world, movement held by another system, no navmesh) prints "nav: <reason>".
  arg x (float): X of the destination.
  arg y (float): Y of the destination - Y is up in EQ2.
  arg z (float): Z of the destination.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.Nav:To[${Me.X},${Me.Y},${Math.Calc[${Me.Z}+30]}]
