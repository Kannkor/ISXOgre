== OgreBotAPI.Position ==

${OgreBotAPI.Position.ActorModelSize[nameOrId]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Position
  what: Returns an actor's model size: its collision radius times its collision scale. Returns 0 when the argument is blank or the actor cannot be found.
  arg nameOrId (string): the actor: an actor id, an @alias or *alias, pet:<owner>, a class word (the first group or raid member it fits) me or your own name (you), or a name (the nearest actor whose name contains it)
  returns: float: the actor's collision radius times its collision scale; 0 when it is not found.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Position.ActorModelSize[${Target.ID}]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.GetActorModelSize

${OgreBotAPI.Position.AtAngle[angle,actorNameOrId,dist=3]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Position
  what: Returns the point at angle degrees around an actor, measured from the actor's heading, dist plus your model size plus the actor's model size away, at the actor's height plus 0.5. Returns "" when the actor cannot be found; it only computes a point and moves nothing.
  arg angle (int): whole degrees around the actor from its heading: 0 is directly behind it and 180 directly in front, 90 and -90 its two sides (a fraction is dropped)
  arg actorNameOrId (string): the actor: an actor id, an @alias or *alias, pet:<owner>, a class word (the first group or raid member it fits) me or your own name (you), or a name (the nearest actor whose name contains it)
  arg dist (float, default: 3): the gap between the two bodies, before the model sizes are added
  returns: string: "x,y,z" with two decimals; "" when the actor is not found.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Position.AtAngle[90,${Target.ID},5]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.PointAtAngle

${OgreBotAPI.Position.AtAngleNoTargetSize[angle,actorNameOrId,dist=3]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Position
  what: Same as Position.AtAngle, but the distance adds only your own model size, not the actor's. Returns "" when the actor cannot be found.
  arg angle (int): whole degrees around the actor from its heading: 0 is directly behind it and 180 directly in front, 90 and -90 its two sides (a fraction is dropped)
  arg actorNameOrId (string): the actor: an actor id, an @alias or *alias, pet:<owner>, a class word (the first group or raid member it fits) me or your own name (you), or a name (the nearest actor whose name contains it)
  arg dist (float, default: 3): the distance before your model size is added
  returns: string: "x,y,z" with two decimals; "" when the actor is not found.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Position.AtAngleNoTargetSize[180,${Target.ID},3]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.PointAtAngleNoSize

${OgreBotAPI.Position.DistanceToActor[nameOrId=Target]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Position
  what: Returns your distance to an actor as the game reports it (the actor's Distance), using your current target when no actor is given. Returns -1 when the actor cannot be found or you are not in the world.
  arg nameOrId (string, default: Target): the actor: an actor id, an @alias or *alias, pet:<owner>, a class word (the first group or raid member it fits) me or your own name (you), or a name (the nearest actor whose name contains it); blank = your current target
  returns: float: the distance to the actor; -1 when it is not found or you are not in the world.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Position.DistanceToActor[${Target.ID}]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.GameDistanceToActor

${OgreBotAPI.Position.FarthestActorFrom[anchorNameOrId,actorName,minDist=20]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Position
  what: Returns the id of the actor named exactly actorName (any case) that is farthest, in straight-line 3D distance, from an anchor actor, as long as it is more than minDist away. Returns 0 when the anchor or no actor of that name is found, or when even the farthest one is not past minDist.
  arg anchorNameOrId (string): the actor to measure from: an actor id, an @alias or *alias, pet:<owner>, a class word or a name (the nearest actor whose name contains it)
  arg actorName (string): the full name of the actors to choose among (whole-name match, any case)
  arg minDist (float, default: 20): the winner must be farther than this from the anchor, or the answer is 0
  returns: int64: the chosen actor's id; 0 when there is none.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Position.FarthestActorFrom[${Target.ID},"a void tentacle",20]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.GetFarthestActorFrom, OgreBotAPI.GetFarthestActorFrom_ref

${OgreBotAPI.Position.FarthestGroupDistance}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Position
  what: Returns the distance to the farthest other member of your group. Returns 0 when you are solo or not in the world.
  returns: float: the largest distance from you to another group member; 0 when there is none.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Position.FarthestGroupDistance}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.GetFarthestGroupDistance, Obj_OgreUtilities.GroupWithinDistance

${OgreBotAPI.Position.MyModelSize}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Position
  what: Returns your model size: your collision radius times your collision scale, the body edge the bot adds to its distances.
  returns: float: your collision radius times your collision scale.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Position.MyModelSize}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.GetMyModelSize

${OgreBotAPI.Position.Toward[fx,fy,fz,tx,ty,tz,dist=15]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Position
  what: Returns the point dist away from the first point in the direction of the second, measured flat (x and z only), with the second point's y. It does not stop at the second point when dist is longer than the gap, and returns "" when fewer than six coordinates are given.
  arg fx (float): the starting point's x
  arg fy (float): the starting point's y (not used in the result)
  arg fz (float): the starting point's z
  arg tx (float): the point to head toward, x
  arg ty (float): the point to head toward, y (becomes the result's y)
  arg tz (float): the point to head toward, z
  arg dist (float, default: 15): how far from the starting point to go
  returns: string: "x,y,z" with two decimals; "" when fewer than six coordinates are given.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Position.Toward[${Me.X},${Me.Y},${Me.Z},${Target.X},${Target.Y},${Target.Z},10]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.PointTowardDist


== OgreBotAPI.Quest ==

${OgreBotAPI.Quest.ActorHas[actorId]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Whether the actor with this id is within a distance of 10 and offers a quest or has a quest update or hand-in for you. With no id it prints a console line and answers FALSE.
  arg actorId (int64): the actor id to check; required
  returns: bool: TRUE when that actor is within 10 and has a quest offer, update or hand-in; FALSE otherwise.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Quest.ActorHas[${Target.ID}]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.ActorHasQuest

${OgreBotAPI.Quest.Have[name]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Whether a quest with this exact name, in any case, is in your active quest journal. No name, or not being in the world, answers FALSE.
  arg name (string): the full quest name to look for; case does not matter
  returns: bool: TRUE when the quest is in your active journal; FALSE otherwise.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Quest.Have["Feathers For Sethis"]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.HaveQuest, Obj_OgreIH.Check_Quest_Exists

OgreBotAPI.Quest:Accept[fw, questName=""]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Clicks Accept on an offered new quest window, never a quest reward window, and does nothing when none is offered. It accepts whether or not the Settings Do not auto accept quests box is ticked, since that box stops only the automatic accept; given a name, a group leader with auto share on shares that quest two seconds later.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  arg questName (string, default: ""): the quest to share after accepting; blank accepts with no share
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:Accept[${Me.Name}]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:AcceptQuest, Obj_OgreUtilities:AcceptQuest

OgreBotAPI.Quest:CheckKunarkAscending[fw]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Opens and closes the quest journal, then prints which Kunark Ascending prerequisite quests you have not completed: the languages, the heritage quest, the signature line and an Epic 1.0 quest. It runs in turn on the action queue and ends with a completed line.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:CheckKunarkAscending[${Me.Name}]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:CheckKunarkAscendingRequirements

OgreBotAPI.Quest:CompareUpdate[fw, questName, sender]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Reads your own steps for a quest and asks other characters to compare theirs; a character on a different step says so in its own console and you get a tally. The character that runs it asks, the for-who is who is asked, and all or blank asks the rest of your group.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: which characters are asked; all or blank is your group but you
  arg questName (string): the quest to compare; a missing one prints a console line
  arg sender (character name): the character that asks; the -CompareQuest console line fills it. Leave it out in a script: the character running it asks, and any other name makes the call do nothing
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:CompareUpdate[all,"Feathers For Sethis"]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:CompareQuestUpdate

OgreBotAPI.Quest:Delete[fw, questName]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Deletes one quest from your own journal straight away and prints the result. There is no group leader check, and a quest that is not in the journal or has moved in it is not deleted.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  arg questName (string): the exact quest name, any case
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:Delete[${Me.Name},"Feathers For Sethis"]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:DeleteQuest, Obj_OgreUtilities:DeleteQuest, Obj_OgreUtilities:DeleteQuestActual

OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShareAllMissions[fw]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Shares every active mission in your journal with the group, waiting up to 5 minutes for the group to gather within 30 and then 3 seconds between shares. With all only the group leader shares, and it says so in the console when you are not grouped or have no missions.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShareAllMissions[all]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:ShareAllMissions

OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShareMission[fw, questName]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Like :ShareQuest, but the quest must be a mission (its journal category starts with Mission). It says so in the console when it is not an active mission, the name is blank or you are not grouped.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  arg questName (string): the exact mission name, any case
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShareMission[all,"Feathers For Sethis"]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:ShareMission

OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShareQuest[fw, questName]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Shares one quest from your journal with the group, after waiting up to 5 minutes for the group to gather within 30. With all only the group leader shares, and it says so in the console when the name is blank, the quest is not in your journal or you are not grouped.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  arg questName (string): the exact quest name, any case
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShareQuest[all,"Feathers For Sethis"]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:ShareQuest

OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShareQuestWhenAble[fw, questName, zoneName]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Shares one quest once the group has gathered (waiting up to 5 minutes), but only while you are in zoneName - leaving that zone first stops the share with a console line. With all only the group leader shares.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  arg questName (string): the exact quest name, any case
  arg zoneName (string): the zone the share is for; blank means the zone you are in now
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShareQuestWhenAble[all,"Feathers For Sethis","${Zone.Name}"]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:ShareQuestWhenAble

OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShowMissionInfo[fw]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Opens the Zone Reuse window's Hot Zones tab and prints one line to the console: the newest expansion's hot zone (each zone named once, without [Heroic] / [Solo]) and the time until it changes, or that the game's list has just expired. It runs on every addressed character, with no group leader check.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShowMissionInfo[${Me.Name}]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:DisplayEtherealMissionInfo

OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShowWiki[fw, name=""]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Opens the EverQuest 2 wiki page for a quest in your web browser; a blank name uses the quest selected in the quest journal, and with none selected a console line says so and nothing opens. A blank for-who means is1 here, not all.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  arg name (string, default: ""): the quest to look up; blank uses the quest selected in the journal
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:ShowWiki[${Me.Name},"Feathers For Sethis"]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:ShowWikiForQuest

OgreBotAPI.Quest:SpewMissionTimers[fw, ...searches]
  kind: method of OgreBotAPI.Quest
  what: Opens the quest journal on its Mission Timers tab and sends one oc line per mission whose name contains a search word, with the time to reset and whether it is completed. With no search word it prints a reminder and nothing else, and it closes the journal when done.
  arg fw (for-who): for-who: this runs only when this character is one it names
  arg ...searches (string): one or more words; a mission matches when its name contains any of them, in any case
  example: OgreBotAPI.Quest:SpewMissionTimers[${Me.Name},Weekly,Daily]
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI:Spew_MissionTimerInfo


== OgreBotAPI.Roster ==

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.ArchetypeOf[class]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: The archetype of a subclass name, in lower case: fighter, priest, mage or scout. An actor ID is also accepted and that actor's class is used; a class tier name such as warrior, or anything unknown, reads an empty string.
  arg class (string): A subclass name such as templar (any case), or an actor ID.
  returns: string - fighter, priest, mage or scout, empty when unknown.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.ArchetypeOf[${Target.ID}]}
  oldbot: ClassInfo.Archetype, GetArchetypeFromClass, GetInfoOb.Archetype

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.BaseClassOf[class]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: The class tier of a subclass name, in lower case: templar reads cleric, berserker reads warrior. An actor ID is also accepted and that actor's class is used; anything that is not a subclass reads an empty string.
  arg class (string): A subclass name such as berserker (any case), or an actor ID.
  returns: string - the class tier (warrior, cleric, bard and so on), empty when unknown.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.BaseClassOf[berserker]}
  oldbot: GetInfoOb.GetBaseClass, GetInfoOb.GetClass, OgreBotAPI.Get_BaseClass

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.CountGroupBy[mode,search]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: Counts the members of your group, you included, that match mode and search. Only player characters count: a member whose type reads as anything else (a corpse, for example) is skipped; with no mode it matches by Class, and a mode word the list does not know matches nobody.
  arg mode (string, default: Class): How to match: All (everyone; search ignored), Class (exact subclass, e.g. templar), RawClass or ParentClass (class tier, e.g. cleric), Archetype, NotArchetype, Role (tank, priest, enchanter, bard, dps), Cursed (search ignored), KraletusCharmers (coercers and troubadors, search ignored). Empty means Class; any other word matches nobody.
  arg search (string): The class, tier, archetype or role name to match, any case.
  returns: int - number of matching group members, 0 when none match.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.CountGroupBy[Archetype,priest]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.CountGroupBy, GetInfoOb.CountGroupBy_, CheckGroupSetup

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.CountRaidBy[mode,search]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: Counts the members of your raid, you included, that match mode and search; in a raid every member counts, corpses too. When you are not in a raid it answers exactly as CountGroupBy does.
  arg mode (string, default: Class): How to match: All, Class, RawClass or ParentClass, Archetype, NotArchetype, Role, Cursed, KraletusCharmers. Empty means Class; any other word matches nobody.
  arg search (string): The class, tier, archetype or role name to match, any case.
  returns: int - number of matching raid members (group members when not in a raid).
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.CountRaidBy[Role,priest]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.CountRaidBy, GetInfoOb.CountRaidBy_

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.Flagged[n=1]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: TRUE when flag number n is set on this character. Flags are kept by this session only, are set with Roster:Flag, and can be read anywhere, even outside the world; flag 24 is the same state as Marked.
  arg n (int, default: 1): The flag number to check.
  returns: bool - TRUE when flag n is set.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.Flagged[2]}
  oldbot: GetInfoOb.FlaggedToon, GetInfoOb:ToonFlagged, OgreBotAPI.ToonFlagged

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.GroupIDBy[mode,search,rank=1]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: The actor ID of the rank-th group member, you included, that matches mode and search, with matches sorted alphabetically by name. Only player characters are considered (a corpse is skipped); reads 0 when nobody matches or rank is past the last match.
  arg mode (string, default: Class): How to match: All, Class, RawClass or ParentClass, Archetype, NotArchetype, Role, Cursed, KraletusCharmers. Empty means Class; any other word matches nobody.
  arg search (string): The class, tier, archetype or role name to match, any case.
  arg rank (int, default: 1): Which match to return, counting from 1 in name order.
  returns: int64 - the member's actor ID, 0 when there is no such match.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.GroupIDBy[Class,templar,1]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.GetGroupIDBy, GetInfoOb.GetGroupIDBy_

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.GroupIDPreferred[search,rank=1]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: The actor ID of the rank-th group member, you included, whose archetype or role matches search, ordered by how useful that class is to spare (for dps the order is flipped so pure damage classes come first). Only player characters are considered (a corpse is skipped), a member matching any of the listed pools counts, ties go to the pool listed first and then to name, and it reads 0 when nobody matches.
  arg search (string): One or more archetypes or roles separated by |, for example scout, dps or scout|mage.
  arg rank (int, default: 1): Which match to return, counting from 1 in preferred order.
  returns: int64 - the member's actor ID, 0 when there is no such match.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.GroupIDPreferred[scout|mage,1]}
  oldbot: GetInfoOb.GetGroupIDPreferred_, OgreBotAPI.GetGroupIDPreferred

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.GroupNameBy[mode,search,rank=1]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: The name of the rank-th group member, you included, that matches mode and search, with matches sorted alphabetically by name. Only player characters are considered (a corpse is skipped), as in GroupIDBy, so the same rank names the same member in both; reads an empty string when nobody matches or rank is past the last match.
  arg mode (string, default: Class): How to match: All, Class, RawClass or ParentClass, Archetype, NotArchetype, Role, Cursed, KraletusCharmers. Empty means Class; any other word matches nobody.
  arg search (string): The class, tier, archetype or role name to match, any case.
  arg rank (int, default: 1): Which match to return, counting from 1 in name order.
  returns: string - the member's name, empty when there is no such match.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.GroupNameBy[Class,templar,1]}
  oldbot: GetInfoOb.GetGroupInformation, GetInfoOb.GetGroupNameBy_, OgreBotAPI.GetGroupNameBy

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.GroupNamePreferred[search,rank=1]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: The name of the rank-th group member, you included, whose archetype or role matches search, in the same preferred order as GroupIDPreferred, so the same rank names the same member in both (a corpse is skipped). search can list several pools with |; reads an empty string when nobody matches.
  arg search (string): One or more archetypes or roles separated by |, for example scout, dps or scout|mage.
  arg rank (int, default: 1): Which match to return, counting from 1 in preferred order.
  returns: string - the member's name, empty when there is no such match.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.GroupNamePreferred[dps,1]}
  oldbot: GetInfoOb.GetGroupNamePreferred_, OgreBotAPI.GetGroupNamePreferred

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.InCombat}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster

Reads NULL when you are not in the world; for your character alone, hated included, use ${OgreBotAPI.Combat.InCombat}.
  returns: bool - TRUE when any member of your group, you included, is in combat mode; FALSE when nobody is.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.InCombat}

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.InEQ2Chars[speaker,fullAccountOnly=FALSE]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: TRUE when speaker is on the list the chat commands trust: every character saved in your account list, on any account (characters not yet assigned to one included), and the name OgreIRCSession, matched in any case. With fullAccountOnly TRUE it is TRUE only when that character also has complete account details saved, the kind a login needs; it works outside the world.
  arg speaker (string): The character name to look up.
  arg fullAccountOnly (bool, default: FALSE): TRUE to require complete saved account details for that character, not just its name on the list.
  returns: bool - TRUE when the name is on that list.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.InEQ2Chars[${Target.Name}]}
  oldbot: OgreBotAPI.InEQ2Chars

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.InRaid}

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.InZone[within=0]}

  arg within (float, default: 0): The farthest a member may be from you. 0 skips the distance check.
  returns: bool - TRUE when the whole group is here.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.InZone[50]}

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.NearestOfClass[class,range=50,scope=auto,includeMe=TRUE]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: The actor ID of a group or raid member of the given class who is within range, or 0 when there is none. A match is the subclass, its class tier or its archetype (warrior finds a berserker, fighter finds any fighter); you are checked first and returned with no distance test when includeMe is TRUE, then the closest member in range wins.
  arg class (string): A subclass, class tier or archetype name, any case. Empty reads 0.
  arg range (float, default: 50): The farthest a member may be. Members whose distance reads 0 are never matched.
  arg scope (string, default: auto): auto (your raid when in one, else your group), raid, or any other word for your group.
  arg includeMe (bool, default: TRUE): TRUE to answer with your own ID when your class matches.
  returns: int64 - the matching member's actor ID, 0 when none is in range.
  example: echo ${OgreBotAPI.Roster.NearestOfClass[priest,30,auto,FALSE]}
  oldbot: GetInfoOb.Class

${OgreBotAPI.Roster.RaidIDBy[mode,search,rank=1]}
  kind: member of OgreBotAPI.Roster
  what: The actor ID of the rank-th raid member, you included, that matches mode and search, sorted alphabetically by name; in a raid every member is considered, corpses too. When you are not in a raid it answers exactly as GroupIDBy does, and it reads 0 when nobody matches.
  arg mode (string, default: Class): How to match: All, Class, RawClass or ParentClass, Archetype, NotArchetype, Role, Cursed, KraletusCharmers. Empty means Class; any other word matches nobody.

  arg num|name (number or text): The reward to pick: its number on the window (1 = the first), or its item name (trimmed, any case, first match). Empty or 0 picks nothing.
  arg windowId (int, default: 0): The reward window id to read. Empty or 0 = the active reward window (the oldest one the bot saw open that is still up).
