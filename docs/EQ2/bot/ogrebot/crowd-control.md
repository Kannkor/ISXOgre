> **:warning: Not finished**
>
> OgreBot marks this tab as not finished (its name is struck through). Some options below work and some do not yet - each one says which.

Crowd Control is not active yet in the new bot. Its settings are saved with your profile, but nothing uses them, which is why the tab is struck through in the tab list. When it is built, the bot will mez (or root) extra mobs that are attacking your group, using the abilities in the Priority Order list, top first, and leaving the mob you are killing alone.

[![Crowd Control tab](../assets/ogrebot/tab-crowd-control.png)](../assets/ogrebot/tab-crowd-control.png "Click for full size")

| Option | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Full Ability list** | Your abilities, with a search box. Click one and press Add (or double-click it) to put it in the Priority Order list. | filled from your ability export |
| **Priority Order** | **Not active yet.** It will be the mez abilities to use, top first.<br>*Drag to reorder, double-click to switch a row off, x to remove. Clicking a row loads it back into the form.* | empty |

## Crowd Control

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Allow Epics as Valid Targets** | **Not active yet.** It will let the bot mez epic mobs as well as ordinary ones. | off |
| **Add** | Adds the ability you clicked on the left to the bottom of the Priority Order list. Next to it you see the picked ability, or 'click an ability on the left' until you pick one. |  |

## Good to know

- Nothing on this tab does anything yet, and neither does 'Enable Crowd Control' on Settings.
- You can still set up the list now: it is saved with your profile and will be used once the feature is built.
