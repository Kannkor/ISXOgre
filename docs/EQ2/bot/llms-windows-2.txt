# Zone Reset

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> Shows your zone lockout timers and resets them, for this character or for your group or fleet.
>
> **What you can do here**
>
> - **Refresh** - read the zone list and timers from the game again.
> - **Click a zone** on the left to queue it; click it on the right to remove it. The **Add** buttons queue many at once.
> - **Show** - tick Solo, Heroic, Raid or Other to choose which zones the list shows. **Sort** - by Status (resettable first), Type (solo, heroic, raid) or Name. These only change the view: the **Add** buttons still look at every zone.
> - **Reset queue for me** - reset every queued zone on this character.
> - **Reset queue via ... for ...** - send the reset to the characters in the Master Control Panel's For Who window.
> - **Force reset** - ctrl-click a queued zone, then press it twice: the first press only arms it, a second press within 5 seconds does the reset.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - Green can be reset, orange is locked (time left in brackets), yellow has no data.
> - **Force reset spends a limited reset charge** and cannot be undone. It only confirms if the game's message names that zone.

**Open it:** Type `ogre zone` (also `ogre reset` or `ogre zonereset`). If the window is already open, this does nothing.

## What it is for

Zone Reset shows your zone lockout timers and resets them for you. It reads the game's Zone Reuse window and lists every zone, coloured by whether it can be reset now, is still locked, or has no data. You pick the zones you want in a reset queue, then reset them on this character, or send the reset to your group or fleet. A separate Force reset button spends one of your limited reset charges on one zone. It needs two clicks, and has a safety check.

## Header and colours

The status text on the right says what the last action did (for example 'scraped 7 zone(s) + 3 with no timer'). 'working…' appears next to the title while it is busy. The colour key explains the zone list: green can be reset, orange is locked with the time left shown in brackets, pale yellow has no data.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Refresh** | Reads the zone list and timers again. It briefly opens the game's Zone Reuse window, reads it, and closes it.<br>*Opening this window with no list yet does a Refresh for you. Nothing happens when you are not in the world.* |

## Full zone list

Every zone the game has a lockout record for: resettable first, then locked, then no data. Zones with 'Raid' in the name carry a small 'raid' tag. Hover a row to see the game's own tooltip for it.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Click a zone** | Adds that zone to the reset queue on the right. A zone already in the queue is not added twice. |
| **Add resettable** | Adds every green (resettable) zone to the queue. |
| **Add non-raid** | Adds every resettable zone that does not have 'Raid' in its name. |
| **Add non-locked** | Adds every zone that is not locked: the resettable ones and the ones with no data. |

## Reset queue

The zones you picked. The queue is kept while the bot is loaded, even if you close this window, but is not saved to disk.

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Click a queued zone** | Removes it from the queue. |  |
| **Ctrl-click (or Alt-click) a queued zone** | Selects that zone as the Force reset target (it is highlighted). Ctrl-click it again to unselect. Display only until you press Force reset. |  |
| **Clear list** | Empties the reset queue. |  |
| **Force reset selected / Force reset: <zone> / Click again to SPEND a charge on: <zone>** | Spends one of your limited zone reset charges to reset the selected zone right now, even if it is locked. It takes two clicks: the first click only arms the button (its text changes to 'Click again to SPEND a charge on: <zone>'), and a second click within 5 seconds does the reset. The bot then opens the Zone Reuse window, finds the zone, clicks Force Reset, and only clicks the confirm button if the game's confirm message names exactly that zone. If it does not, nothing is clicked, no charge is spent, and the confirm window is left open for you.<br>*Cannot be undone. If you do not click again within 5 seconds, the button goes back to normal and nothing is spent.* | greyed out until a zone is selected (and while the window is busy) |
| **Reset queue for me** | Resets the lockout timer of every queued zone on this character only, one zone every half second.<br>*Greyed out while the window is busy. With an empty queue it just says the queue is empty.* |  |
| **Reset queue via <oc/irc> for <who>** | Sends a reset for every queued zone to the characters picked in the Master Control Panel's For Who window, over the bot console (or IRC if that is what the For Who window says). Each character that receives it resets its own timer for those zones. The button text shows where it will go.<br>*If the For Who window has never been set, this goes to every character (all).* | oc for all (when the For Who window has never been set) |

## Footer

A reminder of how the lists work, and that Force reset spends a limited charge.

## What the window shows

- Green zone: its lockout can be reset now (the game's tooltip says so).
- Orange zone with [time]: locked; the time in brackets is how long is left.
- Pale yellow zone: no timer data (the game knows the zone but shows no timer for it).
- 'raid' tag: the zone name contains 'Raid'. The Add non-raid button skips these.
- Status text (top right): what the last action did, for example 'reset 3 queued zone(s)', 'the reset queue is empty', 'not in world', or 'ABORTED: ...' when the Force reset safety check refused.
- 'working…': a refresh or reset is running. The reset buttons are greyed out until it finishes.

## Good to know

- Force reset spends a real, limited reset charge and cannot be undone. The first click only arms it; the reset happens on a second click within 5 seconds. The ordinary 'Reset queue' buttons do not spend charges.
- The fleet button uses the For Who window from the Master Control Panel, not your group. If that window was never set, it resets for every character.
- Refresh and Force reset briefly open the game's Zone Reuse window. That can cause a short stutter. A queue reset opens it too, but only on a character whose game has not loaded its zone list yet.
- The queue is forgotten when the bot is unloaded.

# OSA

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> The On Screen Assistant: the NPCs around you with health, your threat, distance and who they are attacking. Named first, then nearest first - up to 35 rows, and every named in range comes before any other NPC.
>
> **What you can do here**
>
> - **Click a row** to target that NPC. The green row is the one you are fighting.
> - **Gear** - choose the columns (HP, Threat, Distance, Target), then **Apply**.
> - Set how far it looks, "targets only" and Clip Y Axis in OgreBot, HUDs tab, OSA section.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - It opens and closes with **Use On Screen Assistant (OSA)** on OgreBot's Settings tab; `ogre osa` also opens it (and does nothing if it is already open).
> - THR is your threat in percent; at 100 it shows your lead over the next player.
> - Clip Y Axis hides NPCs 8 or more above or below you - the floors over and under you.

**Open it:** Tick **Use On Screen Assistant (OSA)** on OgreBot's Settings tab - it opens at once, opens every time that profile loads, and closes when you untick it. You can also type `ogre osa`.

## What it is for

A compact list of the NPCs around you: name, health, your threat, distance, and who each one is attacking. Named mobs are listed first, then the rest nearest first. Click a row to target that NPC. How far it looks, whether it hides NPCs that are not fighting and whether it hides NPCs on other floors are set in OgreBot, on the HUDs tab (OSA section).

## Title bar

The title shows how many NPCs are listed, for example OSA — 9 NPCs.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⚙ (gear)** | Opens and closes the column picker at the top of the window. |
| **Title bar close (X)** | Closes the window. The OgreBot box stays ticked; type `ogre osa` to bring it back.<br>*To turn OSA off for good, untick the box in OgreBot.* |

## The list

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Click a row** | Targets that NPC. |

## Column picker

Opened with the gear. Name is always shown.

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **HP%** | Shows the HP column: the NPC's health in percent. | on |
| **Threat** | Shows the THR column: your threat on that NPC in percent. When you are at the top of its hate list (100), it shows how far ahead of the next player you are instead. | on |
| **Distance** | Shows the DIST column: how far away the NPC is, in metres. | on |
| **Target** | Shows the TARGET column: the name of whoever that NPC is targeting. Empty means it is not targeting anyone. | on |
| **Apply** | Uses the ticked columns, closes the picker, and remembers the choice for next time.<br>*Ticking a box does nothing until you press Apply.* |  |

## What the window shows

- Rows: NPCs and named NPCs only - no players, pets, corpses or objects. Named first, then everyone else, each group nearest first. At most 35 rows - every named in range is listed before any other NPC, so a far-away named is never the one cut.
- Green row with bold green name: the NPC you are fighting - your target, or your target's target if you have a friend targeted.
- HP: health in percent. THR: your threat in percent, or your lead over the next player once you are at 100. DIST: distance in metres. TARGET: who that NPC is targeting.
- 'no NPCs in range' when nothing is inside the scan radius.
- It refreshes 5 times a second.

## Good to know

- Scan Radius, Display NPCs with Targets Only and Clip Y Axis (OgreBot, HUDs tab) only apply while the Use On Screen Assistant box is ticked. If you open OSA with `ogre osa` and the box is not ticked, it looks 50 metres out and lists every NPC on every floor.
- Scan Radius is capped at 200 metres.
- Clip Y Axis (only show mobs on the same floor as you), in the same OgreBot section, hides every NPC whose height differs from yours by 8 or more.
- `ogre osa` when OSA is already open does nothing and prints nothing.

# NPC Cast Monitoring

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> Two tabs. **Monitor** writes down every spell a mob casts, with its name and spell number. **Watch list** watches the mobs listed for your current zone, and when one starts a listed spell, the bot can interrupt it or run your commands.
>
> **Monitor**
>
> - **Start** / **Stop** - turns the log on and off. It keeps running while you fight, zone or die, until you press Stop.
> - **my target + its target** - logs your target and your target's target. These are the only spells that come with a **name**, because the name is read off your cast bars. Target the mob, or target the tank the mob is attacking.
> - **+ named NPCs nearby** / **+ every NPC nearby** - also logs other casters within 100 metres. Their spells show a spell number but no name, unless you have seen that spell on your cast bar before.
> - **Copy** - puts the whole list on your clipboard, ready to paste.
> - **Clear** - empties the list for this zone.
> - Each zone has its own list, saved to the file named under it. Send that file, or paste the copied list, to whoever asked for it.
>
> **Watch list**
>
> - **Add the spell my target is casting** - press it while your target's cast bar is up.
> - **Add by hand** - type a mob name and a spell number.
> - **interrupt** - the bot interrupts that spell with an interrupt you have ready.
> - **label** / **commands** - opens a small editor under the spell. Type, then **Save** or **Cancel**.
> - **remove** / **remove mob** - stop watching a spell or a whole mob here.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - The Watch list only works when **NPC Cast Monitoring** is ticked on OgreBot's Settings tab. While it is off, a red bar tells you when a button cannot do anything. Interrupts and commands also need OgreBot open and not paused. The Monitor needs none of that.
> - **Scan radius** takes 5 to 500. Leave it empty to use the default.
> - Your changes go in your own list, so updates never remove them. **Reset my edits** deletes them all for this zone, with no undo.

**Open it:** Type `ogre npccast` (also `ogre npccastmonitoring`, `ogre npccast panel` or `ogre npccast config`). If the window is already open, this does nothing. `ogre npccast status` prints the watch status in the console instead.

## What it is for

This window has two tabs. **Monitor** writes down every spell a mob casts, with the spell's name and its ability id, so you can copy the list or send the file to whoever asked for it. It only records; it never makes the bot do anything. **Watch list** watches the mobs listed for the zone you are in, found by their exact name. When a watched mob starts casting one of the listed spells, the bot can interrupt it, run your own commands, or both. Each zone has a shipped list that comes with the bot, and your own list on top of it. Everything you change on the Watch list goes into your own list, so a bot update never removes it. The window opens on the Monitor tab.

## Header and tabs

The top line shows the zone and a count. On the Monitor tab the count is how many spells are in the log. On the Watch list tab it is how many mobs and spells are listed and how many mobs are being watched right now.

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Monitor** | Shows the Monitor tab: the log of what mobs cast. Display only - changes what you see, not what the bot does.<br>*The window always opens on this tab.* | shown |
| **Watch list** | Shows the Watch list tab: the mobs and spells watched in this zone, and what the bot does about them. Display only - changes what you see, not what the bot does. |  |

## Monitor: what to log, Start and Stop

Pick whose spells to log, then press Start. One of the three buttons is always lit. Your target and your target's target are logged in every choice.

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **my target + its target** | Logs the spells of your target and of your target's target. Players are left out. These are the only spells that come with a name, because the name is read off your cast bars. Target the mob, or target the tank the mob is attacking.<br>*Your choice is not saved. After you restart the game it is back to this one.* | on |
| **+ named NPCs nearby** | Also logs every named NPC within 100 meters of you. Their spells show the ability id but no name, unless that spell was once named from your cast bars.<br>*Changing this while the Monitor runs takes effect at once.* | off |
| **+ every NPC nearby** | Also logs every NPC within 100 meters of you, named or not. Their spells show the ability id but no name, unless that spell was once named from your cast bars. | off |
| **Start / Stop** | Start begins logging and the button turns into a red Stop. It checks four times a second and keeps going while you fight, zone or die, until you press Stop. Stop saves the log and stops; the list stays on screen.<br>*Closing the window does not stop it. It also runs while OgreBot is closed or paused, and with NPC Cast Monitoring unticked.* |  |

## Monitor: the log

One row per mob and spell, in the order they were first seen. A grey bar above the log says what your last button press did. With either of the 'nearby' choices, a second grey bar explains why some spells have no name.

## Monitor: Copy, Clear and the file

Under the log: whether the Monitor is running ('monitoring') or 'stopped', the two buttons, and the file the log is saved to.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy** | Puts the whole log on your clipboard as text, ready to paste: a line with the zone name, a line naming the columns, then one line per row (mob \| spell \| ability id \| cast time \| seen). The grey bar says how many lines were copied.<br>*Greyed out while the log is empty.* |
| **Clear** | Empties the log for this zone, and its saved file too. Spell names already learned are kept.<br>*No confirmation and no undo. Greyed out while the log is empty.* |

## Watch list tab

The Watch list always shows the zone you are in right now. Everything below is on this tab.

## Watch list: header

On this tab the zone name is the name of the zone's list file. The counts on the right say how many mobs and spells are listed and how many mobs are being watched right now.

## Watch list: adding spells

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Add the spell my target is casting** | Target a mob while its cast bar is up and press this. The mob's name and the spell it is casting are added to your list for this zone. The result shows in a green (worked) or red (did not work) bar under the buttons.<br>*The spell is added with no label and no action, so nothing happens yet. Tick interrupt or add commands afterwards.* |  |
| **mob name (box)** | The name of the mob to add, exactly as the game shows it. Capital letters do not matter.<br>*This box is not cleared after adding (the number and label boxes are), so you can add several spells for the same mob.* | empty |
| **ability id (box)** | The spell's number (its ability id). Anything that is not a digit is removed. Cleared after adding. | empty |
| **label (optional) (box)** | A name you give the spell so you know what it is. Only shown in this window. Cleared after adding. | empty |
| **Add by hand** | Adds the typed mob and spell number to your list for this zone. Nothing happens if the mob name or the number is empty.<br>*If that mob already has that spell in your list, nothing changes (the label is not updated).* |  |

## Watch list: zone settings and files

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Scan radius (box)** | How far away (in meters) mobs are watched in this zone. The grey number is the value in use when the box is empty. | empty (50) |
| **Set** | Saves the scan radius into your list for this zone. It must be from 5 to 500. An empty box or 0 removes your value, so the shipped value (or 50) is used again.<br>*A number outside 5-500 is refused: a red bar says so and nothing is saved. Check the footer's 'scanning to' number for the value in use.* |  |
| **scan players instead of NPCs** | When ticked, this zone watches players instead of NPCs. Saved into your list for this zone at once.<br>*Only useful for testing in a guild hall with other players casting.* | off |
| **Reload** | Reads the lists for the zone you are in again from disk. Use it after you edit a list file by hand. |  |
| **Import Classic custom** | Copies the converted custom lists from OgreBot Classic into your own list folder. A zone you already have your own list for is skipped, never overwritten. The console says how many files were copied and skipped.<br>*This works even when NPC Cast Monitoring is turned off.* |  |
| **Reset my edits** | Deletes your own list for this zone, so only the shipped list is left. Everything you added, removed or changed in this zone is gone, including the scan radius and the players setting.<br>*There is no confirmation and no undo.* |  |

## Watch list: messages

Bars under the buttons tell you what happened. Green or red is the result of your last capture or edit: when something you pressed could not be done (monitoring is off, no zone is loaded, a scan radius out of range, a list file that cannot be read or written), a red bar says why. Other red bars are problems with a list file. Grey bars are notes.

## Watch list: the list

One block per mob. Under each mob is one row per spell. A yellow 'yours' tag means the mob or spell comes from your own list. 'radius N' is a distance set just for that mob. 'watch only' means the mob has no spells listed. A long list of commands is cut short on its row; hold the mouse over it to read all of it.

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Mob name / arrow** | Click to fold a mob's spells away or show them again. Display only - changes what you see, not what the bot does.<br>*Folds are forgotten when the window is reopened.* | open |
| **remove mob** | Stops watching this mob in this zone. A mob from your own list is deleted from it. A mob from the shipped list is switched off in your list, so it stays off after updates.<br>*No confirmation. To get a shipped mob back, use Reset my edits (which undoes all your edits in this zone).* |  |
| **interrupt** | When ticked and this mob starts casting this spell, the bot targets that mob and casts an interrupt spell that is ready right now. Saved at once.<br>*Nothing is cast if you have no interrupt ready, or while OgreBot is closed or paused.* | as the list says |
| **label** | Opens a small editor right under this spell's row, with a box holding its current name. Type a new name and press Save. Cancel closes the editor without saving.<br>*Saving an empty box removes the name you gave it. A spell from the shipped list then shows its shipped name again.* |  |
| **commands** | Opens a small editor right under this spell's row, with the commands the bot runs each time this mob starts this spell, one per line. The row shows how many commands are set and what they are.<br>*Commands only run while OgreBot is open and not paused.* |  |

## Watch list: editing a label or commands

Pressing label or commands opens this editor under the spell's row.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Editor box** | The name or the command lines you are editing. What you type is kept while the list refreshes. Only one editor is open at a time; opening another one drops what you had not saved.<br>*Pressing Enter does not save. Use the Save button.* |
| **Save** | Saves the editor's text into your own list for this zone and closes the editor. For commands, empty lines are skipped, and saving an empty box removes your commands; a spell from the shipped list then uses its shipped commands again. |
| **Cancel** | Closes the editor. Nothing is saved. |
| **remove** | Removes this spell from the mob. A spell from your own list is deleted. A shipped spell is switched off in your list.<br>*Removing the last spell of a mob you added removes that mob too. A mob from the shipped list stays watched.* |

## Watch list: footer

Shows how far the bot scans and for what (NPCs or players), and where the shipped list and your own list for this zone are stored on disk.

## What the window shows

- Monitor header: the zone the log belongs to - the same name the Watch list tab shows - and 'N spell(s) logged', the number of rows in the log.
- Monitor log columns: Mob, Spell (its name, or a grey '(no name yet)'), Ability ID, Cast time (seconds, from the latest cast) and Seen (how many times that mob cast it). One cast counts once, even though the Monitor sees it several times while the cast bar is up. Casting the same spell again counts again.
- Monitor grey bars: the result of your last Start, Stop, Copy or Clear. With either 'nearby' choice, a second bar says that casts from mobs that are not your target (or your target's target) only get an ability id.
- Monitor footer: 'monitoring' in green while it runs, 'stopped' when it does not, and 'saved to' the log file for this zone.
- Zone name (both tabs): the file name used for the zone, for example Castle_Vacrul_Caverns_of_the_Forsaken_Heroic_II. '(no zone)' when nothing is loaded.
- 'N mob(s) · N ability entr(ies) · watching N': how many mobs and spells are listed, and how many mobs the scan is watching right now.
- Green bar: your last capture worked (or that spell was already in your list). Red bar: your last capture or edit did not work, with the reason (no target, not casting, monitoring off, radius out of range, and so on).
- Red 'OFF' bar: NPC Cast Monitoring is turned off on OgreBot's Settings tab. The list is empty and nothing is watched.
- Grey 'OgreBot is closed' bar: mobs are still watched, but nothing is interrupted and no commands run until OgreBot is open.
- Grey 'Per-cast logging is off' bar: shows until you tick NPCCastDebug on OgreBot's Debug tab.
- Red file bars: the shipped list or your list for this zone cannot be read, your file uses the old 'nomerge' spelling, or some spell numbers in it are not valid and were skipped.
- Grey 'REPLACES' bar: your file is set to replace the shipped list instead of adding to it.
- Mob tags: 'yours' = from your list, 'radius N' = a distance set just for that mob, 'watch only' = no spells listed.
- Spell rows: the spell number, its label ('(no label)' if none), the interrupt box, how many commands it runs, and a 'yours' tag when it comes from your list.
- Watch list footer: 'scanning to N for NPCs/players' is the widest distance in use (the zone radius or the biggest per-mob radius). 'shipped:' and 'yours:' are the two list files for this zone.

## Good to know

- The Monitor and the Watch list are separate. The Monitor only writes down what mobs cast; it never interrupts or runs anything, and it needs neither the NPC Cast Monitoring checkbox nor OgreBot open.
- Spell names in the Monitor only come from your cast bars (your target and your target's target), and only when the cast lasts longer than about a quarter of a second. Once a spell's name is known, it shows for that spell everywhere, in every zone and next time too.
- Each zone keeps its own Monitor log. Zone while it runs and the old zone's log is saved and the new zone's saved log comes back. Send the file named under the log, or paste what Copy gives you.
- The Monitor keeps running after you close the window. Press Stop (or type `ogre npccast monitor off`) to end it. The console can also start it: `ogre npccast monitor`, `ogre npccast monitor named` or `ogre npccast monitor all`.
- The Watch list is switched by the 'NPC Cast Monitoring' checkbox on OgreBot's 'Settings' tab. While it is off, the list is empty and capturing, reloading, adding, removing and editing are refused with a red bar saying so. Only Import still works.
- Entering a zone that has a list (shipped or yours) ticks that checkbox for you, once per zone. If you untick it, it stays off until you next enter a zone that has a list, or your profile is loaded again.
- A watched mob starting a spell that is not listed (or a 'watch only' mob) does not interrupt or run anything. It only lets encounter scripts know the mob is casting.
- Interrupts and commands only happen while OgreBot is open and not paused.
- Reset my edits deletes all your Watch list edits for this zone at once, with no confirmation.
- The Watch list only covers the zone you are standing in. To edit another zone's list, go there or edit its file.
- With NPC Cast Monitoring off, an empty Watch list says so and asks you to turn it on - adding needs it on.

# Effect Tracker

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> Records what effects are called and what they say they do, into a shared per-zone data set for encounter writers.
>
> **What you can do here**
>
> - **Player** - record the detrimentals that land on you.
> - **NPC** - record the effects on NPCs that are fighting.
> - **Merge zone** - combine every character's capture file for the zone you are in into one. It works with Player and NPC off too.
> - **Search** and click a row to read everything recorded for an effect.
> - **Right-click a row** to copy its coding details.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - Both start off in every new session. The window opens shrunk; click **Expand**.

## What it is for

Effect Tracker records what effects are called and what they say they do. With Player on, it records every detrimental that lands on you. With NPC on, it records the effects on NPCs that are fighting. It opens each new effect's examine window, copies its text, and closes it again. Each effect is only looked at once, ever, and the results go into a shared per-zone data set that encounter writers use. This window turns the capture on and off and lets you browse what was captured in the zone you are in.

The green line shows the zone, your character, how many effects are waiting to be examined, and the name of the last effect captured.

- Header line: zone file name, your character, 'N/8 examining' while effects wait to be read, and 'last:' the last effect captured.

# Radar

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>

# Nav

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> The stop button for navigation. It pops up whenever the bot walks or flies your character somewhere, and closes when the trip ends.
>
> **What you can do here**
>
> - **STOP** - end the trip where you are. Safe to press at any time.
> - **Pause / Resume** - hold the trip and keep the route; Resume carries on from here.
> - **Close** - hide the window; the trip keeps going.
> - Watch the progress bar, waypoint count and distance left. **Waiting for mesh** means the zone's map is still loading; the trip starts by itself when it is ready.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - Pausing OgreBot does not stop a trip - use STOP (or `ogre nav stop`).
> - While nav is paused nothing else will move your character until you Resume or STOP.
> - `ogre nav -nopanel` stops it popping up; `ogre nav panel` opens it by hand.

**Open it:** It opens by itself whenever your character starts a navigation trip, whoever started it (you, Instance Controller, the travel router...), and closes when the trip ends. To open it by hand type `ogre nav panel`. `ogre nav -nopanel` stops it popping up; `ogre nav -panel` turns that back on and opens it.

## What it is for

The stop button for navigation. While the bot walks or flies your character somewhere along the navigation map, this small window shows how far along it is and gives you a big STOP and a Pause. Navigation keeps going while OgreBot is paused, so this window is how you stop it.

## Status

The top line is the state (Moving in green, Failed in red, Arrived highlighted, Stopped plain) and why. The bar and the line under it show the waypoint you are on out of the whole route, and the distance still to go. The next line names the navigation map loaded for this zone. A red note appears when the trip is paused, nav is switched off, or OgreBot is paused.

## Buttons

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **STOP** | Ends the trip right where you are. The route is thrown away. It is safe to press at any time: if something else has since taken over moving your character, STOP leaves that alone.<br>*Greyed out when no trip is running.* |
| **Pause / Resume** | Pause stops walking but keeps the route, so Resume carries on from where you stopped without planning again. While paused, nav still holds the character: follow or the camp spot will not move it until you Resume or STOP.<br>*Greyed out when no trip is running. Turns yellow and reads Resume while paused.* |
| **Close** | Closes the window. The trip carries on. The window comes back on the next trip (unless you turned that off with `ogre nav -nopanel`). |
| **Title bar close (X)** | Hides the window, like Close. It comes back on the next trip. |

## What the window shows

- State word: Moving, Planning (the route is being worked out, a little at a time so the game never freezes; the character waits until it is ready), Landing (coming down from flight), Waiting for mesh (the navigation map for this zone is still downloading; the trip starts by itself when it is ready), Arrived, Stopped (something ended the trip on purpose: you pressed STOP, or the bot that started the walk ended it), Failed (navigation could not get there, for example stuck or no route), or Idle.

- Red notes: 'Paused here. The route is kept - Resume carries on from this point.' / 'Nav is switched OFF - `ogre nav -on`' / 'OgreBot is paused. This trip keeps going; STOP ends it.'

## Good to know

- Pausing OgreBot does NOT stop a navigation trip. Use STOP here, or type `ogre nav stop` (also `ogre end nav`).

- While nav is paused it keeps control of your character, so nothing else (follow, camp spot) will move it until you Resume or STOP.

# Safe Fall Trainer

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> Trains Safe Fall for you: mount, fly up, dismount, fall, land, remount, repeat. Every landing is a skill-up chance.
>
> **What you can do here**
>
> - **Flight time** - how long each climb lasts, in ticks (10 ticks = 1 second, 5 to 600).
> - **Start** - target yourself, mount up and begin. It is dimmed, and does nothing, while a run is going or you are not in the world.
> - **Stop** - end training where you are.
> - Watch the phase, rounds, falls trained and ramps as it runs. "started at" is the flight time this run began with.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - Clear your target to stop it; any target (even a mob) keeps it going.
> - Every 100 rounds the flight grows by half a second, up to 25 seconds.
> - Zoning ends the run. Do not mix it with follow, nav or a camp spot.

**Open it:** Type `ogre safefall panel`. `ogre safefall on` (or `ogre safefall on 45` for a 4.5 second flight) starts training without the window, `ogre safefall off` stops it, and a bare `ogre safefall` prints its status in the console.

## What it is for

Trains your Safe Fall skill for you. It targets you, mounts up, then repeats: fly straight up for the flight time, dismount so you fall, wait until you land, remount, and go again. Every landing is a Safe Fall skill-up chance. Every 100 rounds the flight gets half a second longer so the falls get higher. It runs until you stop it, untarget yourself, or leave the world.

## Start, Stop and flight time

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Start** | Starts training with the flight time in the box: targets you, mounts up, waits 7 seconds, then begins the climb-and-fall loop.<br>*Dimmed, and does nothing, while training runs or while you are not in the world. To change the flight time, press Stop first.* |  |
| **Stop** | Stops training and stops the climb. You stay wherever you are (mounted or not).<br>*Dimmed, and does nothing, when nothing is running.* |  |
| **Flight time (… ticks)** | How long each climb lasts, in ticks: 1 tick is 0.1 second, so 30 is 3 seconds (shown to the right). Allowed 5 to 600.<br>*A number outside 5 to 600 turns the box red and Start refuses. The box follows the running value until you type in it; empty it to hand it back.* | 30 ticks (3.0 s) |

## Status

Read-only. It updates four times a second.

## What the window shows

- Pill next to SAFE FALL: 'running' (green), 'idle', or 'not in world' (red).
- Phase: what it is doing now - starting, flying up, stopping climb, dismounting, falling (1/2), falling (2/2), landed, remounting, idle.
- Round: how many rounds since the last flight-time increase, out of 100.
- Falls trained: completed climb-and-fall rounds since you pressed Start.
- Ramps taken: how many times the flight time has grown this run.
- In world / Target / Flying: yes or no. Target means you have something targeted (training stops when you have nothing targeted). Flying means you are flying on your mount.
- Deadline: the current flight time in milliseconds. It never goes above 25000 (25 seconds), even if the ticks keep growing.
- The long line: flight ticks and seconds, the flight time this run started at ('not started yet' before the first Start), the ramp rule, ramps so far and falls so far.
- The note box: the last thing that happened, for example 'dismounted - falling', 'self-target lost', 'left the world'.

## Good to know

- The quickest way to stop is to clear your target: training ends as soon as you have nothing targeted. Clicking a mob does not stop it - any target keeps it going.
- Zoning, camping or going to character select ends the run; it does not pick up again afterwards.
- It does not check whether something else is moving your character. Do not run it together with other movement (follow, nav, camp spot).
- Typing `ogre safefall on` while training is running starts over from round 0 (the Start button is dimmed then, so it cannot do this by accident).
- `ogre safefall panel` when the window is already open does nothing and prints nothing.

# SmartLoot

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> Decides who gets each drop from a Leader Only loot window. You list the items, a cap for each, and which characters collect them.
>
> **What you can do here**
>
> - **Add items** by name, or from the loot window, your bags, or an examine window.
> - **Set a cap** per item: Never, a Limit (up to N each), or unlimited.
> - **Tick a character** to say they collect an item; the number is how many they already have.
> - **+ Character** adds one column by name; **+ Group** and **+ Raid** add a column for everyone in your group or raid who does not have one yet (mercenaries left out). The number on the button is how many it would add.
> - **Save** writes your changes. **Revert** throws them away. Running `ogre smartloot` again while this is open changes nothing.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - A drop goes to the lowest group or raid slot that collects it and is still under the cap.
> - It only runs on a Leader Only loot window, and only when the OgreBot Loot tab has both **Enable options below** (Leader Only) and **Smart Loot** ticked.
> - The x on a character column deletes that character's whole entry when you Save.

**Open it:** Type `ogre smartloot` or `ogre sl`. If the window is already open, this does nothing, and your unsaved changes stay. Scripts can use `OgreBotAPI.Loot:Config[all]` to open it and `OgreBotAPI.Loot:Config[all,close]` to close it.

## What it is for

SmartLoot hands out drops from a Leader Only loot window. It decides who gets each item from per-item caps and a per-character list of what each character collects and how many they already have. This window edits that data: the caps and, for each character, a checkbox plus a have-count. When a Leader Only window opens, SmartLoot goes through the raid (or group), lowest slot first. It gives the item to the first character who collects it and is still under the cap, then adds 1 to their have-count.

## Save and Revert

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Revert** | Throws away every unsaved edit and reloads the caps and the character list from disk. Items added this session and empty character columns disappear.<br>*Greyed out when there are no unsaved changes. Also use it after you fix a damaged file (see the red banner).* |
| **Save** | Saves only what changed: custom caps go to ItemLootCustomData.json, the character list goes to CharacterLootData.json. Your edits are combined with the file as it is now, so have-counts SmartLoot added while the window was open are kept. SmartLoot then starts using the new data right away.<br>*Greyed out when there is nothing to save. If a file cannot be read, it is not saved and the changes stay unsaved.* |

## Filters (what you see)

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Filter items…** | Hides item rows whose name does not contain the text. Not case-sensitive. Display only; nothing is sent to the bot.<br>*Resets when the window is reopened.* | empty |
| **Filter characters (name, *Team:X)…** | Hides character columns. Separate terms with commas. A term is part of a name, a saved for-who team as *Team:Name, or a bare team name. A column shows if any term matches. Display only.<br>*Typing here cancels the single-column view you get by clicking a name. An unknown team shows 'no team named "X"' in the info text. Teams come from the saved for-who groups.* | empty |
| **My group only** | Toggle. Shows only the columns of characters in your current group (mercenaries excluded). When you are solo it shows just you. Display only.<br>*Highlighted when on. Info text says 'not grouped - showing you' when solo.* | off |
| **Show all** | Clears the character filter box, turns off My group only, and clears a clicked-on single column. Display only.<br>*Does not clear the item filter box.* |  |

## Adding items

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Add item by name…** | Text box for an item name. Pressing Enter does the same as Add. | empty |
| **Add** | Adds a row for the typed item name. If the item has no base or custom cap yet, it gets a custom cap of Limit 1. Nobody is marked as collecting it.<br>*The name is not checked against the game, so a typo makes a row that never matches loot. Matching is not case-sensitive. The box clears after you add.* |  |
| **From loot window** | Adds a row for every item in the open loot window, without colour codes in the names. Items with no existing cap get Limit 1.<br>*With no loot window open it adds nothing and the console shows 'add from loot window: 0 item(s)'.* |  |

## Who a change applies to, and characters

| Control | What it does | Default |

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **<character name>** | Click to show only this character's column. Click again to show all. Display only.<br>*Your own name is highlighted.* |  |

## What the window shows

- Title 'SmartLoot — X': X is the character this session is logged into. That column's header is highlighted and is always the first column.
- Dot and 'unsaved changes': orange means some edit is not saved yet. Save and Revert are enabled only then.
- Red banner: appears when a loot file could not be read. It reads '⚠ CharacterLootData.json (ledger) / ItemLootCustomData.json (custom caps) is unreadable — Save is blocked from overwriting it. Fix or remove the file, then Revert.'
- Info text: 'showing X of Y characters' when any column filter is on, plus notes such as 'only Brutus (click the name again to show all)', 'not grouped - showing you', or 'no team named "X"'.
- Rows: items with a custom cap, plus every item any character collects, plus items added this session. It never shows the whole base list. Rows are sorted by name, ignoring case.
- Item column: the item name (hover for the full name), plus the Group and Raid mini buttons.
- Cap column: the segmented control shows the effective cap (custom, else base, else 0). A grey 'default' means the value comes from the base file. An orange 'default N' plus a 'reset' link means a custom value overrides base N (an unlimited base shows as ∞). Nothing extra appears for a custom-only item.
- Character cells: the checkbox means 'this character collects this item'. The number is its have-count.
- How SmartLoot uses it: only on a 'Leader Only' loot window, and only when both OgreBot Loot tab options 'Enable options below' (Leader Only) and 'Smart Loot' are on. It goes through the raid (or group), lowest slot first, checking the item and then its adornments. The first member who collects it, whose cap is not 0, and who is under the cap (or the cap is unlimited) gets it. Their have-count goes up by 1. An item with no cap in either file counts as Never.

## Good to know

- The × on a character column deletes that character's whole SmartLoot entry when you Save: every item they collect and every have-count. It is not a hide, and there is no confirmation.
- The × on an item row unmarks every character from collecting that item. If the item is in the shipped base list, its base cap still applies.
- The scope row (That character / Whole group / Whole raid / Everyone in the file) changes who every checkbox and have-count edit hits. It resets to 'That character' each time the window opens. 'Everyone in the file' also changes characters hidden by the column filters.
- A group or raid edit made while you are not grouped or not in a raid changes nothing. The only message is in the console.
- The per-row Group and Raid buttons always act on your group or raid, whatever the scope row says.
- Sync from inventory only updates the character this session is logged into. It also marks you as collecting every capped item you hold, and never lowers a count for an item you no longer have.
- A newly added character column is not saved until at least one item is checked for it.
- An item with no cap anywhere counts as Never, so nobody gets it even if their box is checked.
- Using reset, or typing a Limit equal to the base cap, can make a row disappear if nobody collects the item.
- Messages from Add, From loot window, From inventory, From examine and group/raid edits go to the console, not the window.
- SmartLoot only hands out items when the OgreBot Loot tab has both 'Enable options below' (Leader Only) and 'Smart Loot' ticked. If they were never set they count as off; the Instance Controller ticks them for the group when it runs a zone.

# Potency / Crit Bonus / Fervor

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> Records your Potency, Crit Bonus and Fervor during fights and shows averages, lows, highs and time at cap. It only measures; it changes nothing.
>
> **What you can do here**
>
> - **Stop / Start** - pause and carry on recording. The numbers are kept.
> - **Reset** - throw away every number and start the session over.
> - **Hover** a figure for more detail.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - **At cap** is when the game says the stat is capped. **At peak** is time at your best value this session.
> - Closing the window does not stop recording; use **Stop** or `ogre potcb off`.

**Open it:** Type `ogre potcb` (or `ogre cbpot`). This opens the window and starts recording. `ogre potcb off` (or `ogre end potcb`) stops recording and closes it.

## What it is for

This window records your Potency, Crit Bonus and Fervor several times a second and shows how they behaved during your fights: the average, lowest and highest value in combat, how much of the time the game said the stat was capped, and how much of the time you were at your best value. It is a measuring tool only; it does not change what the bot does. All the averages are weighted by time, so a slow frame does not count more than a smooth one.

## Start, Stop and Reset

The green text on the left says 'sampling every 200 ms' while recording, or 'stopped'.

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Start / Stop** | Stop pauses the recording. Start carries on from where it stopped; the numbers so far are kept. The time while stopped is not counted. | Stop (recording starts when the window is opened) |
| **Reset** | Throws away every number and starts the session over. Recording keeps going if it was on.<br>*No confirmation.* |  |

## The table

One row per stat. Hover most numbers for more detail.

## What the window shows

- Now: the value right now.
- Combat avg: your average while in combat (hover for how long you have been in combat). Out of combat you are usually unbuffed, so combat is what matters.
- Min / Max: the lowest and highest value seen in combat.
- At cap: the share of combat time the game's own tooltip said the stat had reached its cap.
- At peak: the share of combat time you were at (or within 0.1% of) the highest value seen this session. When a new highest value appears, this starts again from zero, so it is optimistic early on.
- Spread: a small bar chart of your combat time split into ten steps from 0% to 100% of your peak. The right-most (green) bar is time spent near your peak. Hover a bar for its exact share. Like At peak, it starts again when a new peak appears.
- Peak: the highest value seen this session, in or out of combat (hover for how long ago it was set).
- All avg: the average over the whole session, in and out of combat (hover for the overall min and max).
- 'no combat time yet': recording, but you have not been in combat. 'no samples yet': the stat has not been read yet.
- Footer: how long the session has been recording and how much of it was in combat.

## Good to know

- Closing the window with the X does not stop the recording. Use Stop, or `ogre potcb off`.
- Opening the window with `ogre potcb` always starts recording.
- Zoning is not counted as time, so a long zone-in does not inflate 'At peak'.
- The numbers are lost when the bot is unloaded; nothing is saved to a file.

# Level Transmuting

> **:bulb: In game - the ? button on this window says**
>
> Levels your Transmuting skill: buys cheap transmuter's stones from Barnaby Blunderbuss in Butcherblock and transmutes them, round after round.
>
> **What you can do here**
>
> - **Buy** sets how many stones to hold after each visit. Leftover stones are used first.
> - **round(s)** is the most visits one run makes. The run also stops when your skill is capped.
> - **Start** begins the run. **Stop** ends it. **Refresh** redraws the window.
> - **Buys** shows the merchant visits and how many stones they really added.
> - **Transmutes** shows how many stones were tried and how many really got used up. If any failed, it says why: the cast was refused, the ability never became ready, or the stone was still there afterwards.
>
> **Good to know**
>
> - Start pauses OgreBot for the run. When the run ends it resumes OgreBot, unless OgreBot was already paused.
> - Start opens the Persona window on Skills > General to read your skill. It closes it again when the run ends, however it ends.
> - Not next to Barnaby? The window tells you, and Start travels to him first, even with OgreBot closed. Stop ends the trip.
> - Each stone costs 1 Gnomish Currency Exchange, not coin. Without any, the buy adds nothing and the run stops and says so.
> - Only items named "Transmuter's Stone" or "Transmutation Stone" are ever transmuted.
> - A round that buys nothing, or transmutes nothing, stops the run, so a problem never repeats for every round.
> - Closing this window does not stop a run.

**Open it:** Type `ogre leveltransmuting` (or `ogre lt`). Typing it again while the window is open just refreshes it. `ogre lt status` prints the status to the console instead. `ogre end leveltransmuting` stops a run but leaves the window open.

## What it is for

A skill-up grinder for the Transmuting tradeskill. The bot buys Minor Lapis Lazuli Transmuter's Stones from Barnaby Blunderbuss in Butcherblock Mountains, transmutes them, and repeats until your Transmuting skill is capped or the round limit is reached. You do not have to stand next to him: if you are not within 10 of Barnaby, Start travels to him first, from another zone too. OgreBot is paused for the run, and the Persona window is opened on Skills > General to read your skill; when the run ends, however it ends, the window is closed again and OgreBot resumes if it was running. It only ever transmutes items whose name contains "Transmuter's Stone" or "Transmutation Stone" - nothing else in your bags is touched.

## Header and the stones-only notice

The dot is green while a run is going. Next to it is what the bot is doing right now.

## The readout

Merchant and Item are fixed - the tool only works with Barnaby and the Minor Lapis Lazuli Transmuter's Stone. Transmuting is your skill as last read from the Persona window (Start reads it, then it is read again at the start of each round, so it shows unavailable until a run has read it). Rounds counts the rounds started out of the most you allowed. Buys shows the visits to Barnaby and how many stones your bags really gained. Transmutes shows how many stones were tried and how many really got used up. Last is the latest thing that happened.

## Quantity and round limit

| Control | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy** | How many stones you want to be holding when each visit to Barnaby is done. The bot only buys the difference, so stones left over from the last round are used first. 1 to 800.<br>*Locked while a run is going. It is a total to hold, not an amount added every round.* | 60 |
| **per visit, at most ... round(s)** | The hard stop: the most rounds one run will make. Each round is one visit to Barnaby. The run also stops earlier if the Transmuting skill is readable and capped. 1 to 500.<br>*If the skill cannot be read, the run cannot tell when you are capped and goes on until this limit.* | 20 |

## Start, Stop, Refresh

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start** | Starts a run with the two numbers above. It pauses OgreBot and opens the Persona window on Skills > General to read your Transmuting skill. Then, each round: read the skill and stop if it is capped; travel to Barnaby if he is not within 10; buy up to the Buy amount; then transmute every stone in your bags (at most 200 per round), one at a time, waiting 2 seconds after each.<br>*Greyed out while a run is going. The run can also stop by itself - see Good to know below.* |
| **Stop** | Stops the run straight away, even in the middle of buying, travelling or transmuting. It closes the Persona window and resumes OgreBot if it was running before you pressed Start.<br>*Only active while a run is going.* |
| **Refresh** | Redraws the window with the latest numbers. It does not change anything, and it does not read the skill. |
| **Title bar close (X)** | Closes the window only. A run that is going keeps going.<br>*Use Stop (or `ogre end leveltransmuting`) to stop the run.* |

## When the skill cannot be read

The bot reads your Transmuting skill from the Persona window, Skills > General, which Start opens itself. It reads it once when the window opens and again at the start of every round. If the window is closed during a run, the next round opens it again. If the Transmuting row still does not read, Transmuting shows 'unavailable' in orange and a note appears; the run then cannot tell when you are capped and goes on until the round limit, unless a trip, a buy or a transmute fails first and stops it. Before your first run the skill has not been read yet, so the window shows 'unavailable' and a note saying it is read when you press Start.

## What the window shows

- Dot and phase text (top right): green dot = running. Phase is one of idle, waiting for the world, opening the Persona window, travelling to the merchant, reading the Transmuting skill, looking for the merchant, buying, transmuting stones, BLOCKED - cannot transmute, stopped.
- Box at the top: always there. It says the tool transmutes Transmuter's and Transmutation stones only, and that other items are transmuted by the Inventory Manager (`ogre im -tse`).
- Note about Barnaby (only while stopped): shown when you are not within 10 of him. It says you are not in Butcherblock Mountains, how far you are from him, or that he is not in view - and that Start will travel or walk to him first.
- Transmuting: 'current / max' in green from the last read of the Persona window's Skills > General page; 'unavailable' in orange when it could not be read, or before any run has read it.
- Rounds: 'N of M (buy visits finished: B)' - rounds started out of your limit, and how many visits to Barnaby finished their buy.
- Buys: 'N visits, S stones bought' - S is how many stones your bags really gained. It adds 'X added nothing' and turns orange when a visit added no stones.
- Transmutes: 'D of T done' - stones tried, and stones that really left your bags. Green once one is done. When any failed it turns orange and says why: cast refused, never ready, or not used up.
- Last: the latest step or the reason the run stopped, for example 'transmuting 60 stone(s)' or 'Transmuting is at its cap for this level'.
- Footer: a reminder that Start pauses OgreBot, opens the Persona window (Skills > General) and travels to Barnaby when you are not within 10, and that Stop, or the run ending any other way, closes the Persona window and resumes OgreBot if it was running.

## Good to know

- You do not have to stand next to Barnaby Blunderbuss. Start travels to him in Butcherblock Mountains when you are not within 10, even with OgreBot closed. Stop ends the trip. If two trips still leave him out of reach, or one trip takes over 20 minutes, the run stops.
- Start opens the Persona window on Skills > General itself and closes it again when the run ends, however it ends. You do not need to open it first.
- Start pauses OgreBot for the run. When the run ends it resumes OgreBot, unless OgreBot was already paused.
- Each stone costs 1 Gnomish Currency Exchange, not coin. Without any, the buy adds nothing and the run stops and says so.
- Before your first run, Transmuting shows unavailable - the skill is first read when you press Start, and Refresh does not read it.
- Buy is a target to hold, not an amount per round. If stones were left over, the next visit buys fewer (or none).
- Any item in your bags whose name contains "Transmuter's Stone" or "Transmutation Stone" is transmuted, not just the ones this tool bought.
- The run stops by itself when a visit's buy adds no stones, when a round uses up none of the stones it tried, when the merchant window fails 3 rounds in a row, or when you do not have the Transmuting ability (the stones it bought stay in your bags).
- Closing the window does not stop a run.
- `ogre lt` opens the window; `ogre lt status` prints the status to the console instead.

## Before the first run

![Stopped, before the first run](../assets/transmute/idle.png)

Before your first run the skill has not been read, so Transmuting reads **unavailable** and the note says it is read when you press Start: Start opens the Persona window on Skills > General itself. If the Transmuting row still cannot be read during a run, the run cannot tell when you are capped and goes on until the round limit, unless a trip, a buy or a transmute fails first and stops it.
