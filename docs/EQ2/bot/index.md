This guide explains every window of the new OgreBot: what each one is for, and what every option on it does.

Every page starts with a picture of the window, then goes through it one area at a time. The area being
described is outlined in **pink** in its picture.

> **:memo: Examples use made-up names**
>
> Every name in these pictures - Kannkor, Brutus, Mirelle, Tobbin - is made up. Nothing here shows a real
> character, account, guild or server.

## Where to start

- New to the bot? Read [The basics](basics.md) first. It covers the window buttons every window shares.
- Setting up a character: [OgreBot](ogrebot/index.md), then its [Settings](ogrebot/settings.md) and
  [Cast Stack](ogrebot/cast-stack.md) tabs.
- Running zones: the [Instance Controller](windows/ic.md).
- Sharing out loot: [SmartLoot](windows/smartloot.md).

## Help inside the game

Every window has a **?** in its title bar. Click it for a short explanation of that window. The
**Full documentation** button in that box opens this guide.
