== ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset ==

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Add[zone]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Adds one zone to the reset queue and prints how many it holds.
  arg zone (string): The zone's name; several words are joined into one name; required.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Add["The Feerrott"]

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:AddFilter[filter]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Adds every zone the filter picks from the last read to the reset queue and prints how many.
  arg filter (string): resettable, noraid or notlocked. Optional: anything else reads as resettable.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:AddFilter[resettable]

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Clear
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Empties the reset queue.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Clear

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Dump
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Prints the zone table from the last read to the console.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Dump

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:End
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Stops what this tool asked for, drops what it had queued to run, and closes the window, the same as `ogre end zone` and the window's X. A reset that an Instance Controller run or a script asked for carries on.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:End

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Flags[flags]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: The flag form, the same as `ogre zone -zone ...`; -all with -force is refused. -h prints the usage.
  arg flags (string): -zone names one zone and takes every word up to the next flag, -force force resets it and spends a charge, -all resets every resettable zone, -allowRaid includes raid zones, -fleet goes to the For Who selection; one argument each; required.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Flags[-zone,"The Feerrott"]

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Force[zone]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Force resets one zone, which SPENDS one of your limited reset charges and cannot be undone. With no zone it prints one warning and does nothing, and it is refused while a flow runs.
  arg zone (string): The zone's name; several words are joined into one name; required.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Force["The Feerrott"]

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Help
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Prints the `ogre zone` words and flags to the console.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Help

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:List
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Reads the Zone Reuse window again; it opens and closes. Refused while a flow runs.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:List

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Open
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Opens the Zone Resetter window, which reads the Zone Reuse window, the same as bare `ogre zone`.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Open

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Queue
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Prints the reset queue to the console.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Queue

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Reset[word]
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Resets the timer of every zone in the queue. Refused while a flow runs.
  arg word (string): fleet also sends it to the For Who window's selection. Optional.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Reset[fleet]

ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Status
  kind: method of ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset
  what: Prints the flow state, zone and queue counts and counters to the console.
  example: ISXOgreEQ2.ZoneReset:Status
