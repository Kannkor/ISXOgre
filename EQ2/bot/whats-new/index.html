    What's new¶

    OgreBot is in beta testing. Looking for OgreBot Classic? Click here.

    What changed in OgreBot, release by release, newest first. Every release listed here was published to the patcher.

    A release is named by the day it was published and its number that day: 2026.10.16.005 is the fifth release on October 16, 2026. Releases with nothing you would notice are not listed, so the numbers can skip.

    Dates and times are US Eastern time (EST in winter, EDT in summer).

    2026.11.06.002¶

    Friday, November 6, 2026 at 8:44 PM EST

    • Changed · Bot: A missing or out-of-date ability export now loads OgreBot paused. OgreBot used to load running on an old export, or on none. It now loads paused, as classic OgreBot did, and says so in the export prompt. It stays paused after the export finishes, so resume it when you are ready.
    • Changed · Bot: The AbilityViaDebug box on the Debug tab is now Ability query debug. On the Debug tab, the box labelled AbilityViaDebug now reads Ability query debug. It is the same box, and your saved setting carries over.
    • Changed · Bot: The HO icon lookup reads Classic's -No options. OgreBotAPI.Abilities.FindCastableHOIconAbilityID now reads the -No options Classic's Get_HOIconAbility took, such as -NoStun and -NoRoot, on top of your settings boxes. An option it does not use is named once on the console and ignored, where before it was dropped without a word.
    • Changed · Bot: Two more ability calls have names that say what they do. ${OgreBotAPI.Abilities.InterruptID[actor]} is now ${OgreBotAPI.Abilities.FindCastableInterruptID[actor]}, and ${OgreBotAPI.Abilities.HOIconAbility[icon,...]} is now ${OgreBotAPI.Abilities.FindCastableHOIconAbilityID[icon,...]}. Each one finds an ability you can cast right now and gives back its id, without casting it. The arguments are the same and the old names are gone, so change them in your scripts.
    • Changed · Bot: Watcher alerts make their sound, and Track Shiny sees the whole zone. Find now plays the alarm when a listed name appears or leaves, on the session that saw it, as classic OgreBot did. Track Shiny now dings every session when it finds a new shiny, and it looks through the whole zone, nearest first, where it used to stop at 100 metres.
    • Changed · Other: ogre export says where a script's options go. ogre export still takes only -start, -default and stop, and now says where the options go when it refuses a word: a script passes them to ISXOgreEQ2.Export:Abilities, for example ISXOgreEQ2.Export:Abilities[-tradeskill]. The Classic conversion guide says the same, and it now answers for the old words ogre tell, ogre mapper and ogre det.
    • Fixed · Bot: A blank movement key call goes back to your key bind. OgreBotAPI.Movement:SetForwardKey, SetBackwardKey, SetFlyUpKey and SetFlyDownKey with no key now go back to your own key bind; they used to set w, s, Home or End, whatever you had bound. Naming a key works as before, until your key binds next change. A script that named a key before anything had moved no longer leaves the other movement keys unset.
    • Fixed · Bot: A misspelt section name is reported in every data file. Every data file you can layer with your own copy now prints one warning naming a section it does not know, such as "Zones" for "zones", where before the section was skipped without a word. Only that section is skipped, and a name starting with _ is a note. A crafting mats list with no "key" now says it was not used, and a Hide Effects add or remove for a group that does not exist says it did nothing, instead of "skipped".
    • Fixed · Bot: A relay group in the list is one name everywhere. A group in the relay group list is now joined at profile load and left on close under the name you typed. A name with a space or a symbol used to become a squashed copy there, so the toon stayed in the real group after closing. im trade now gives the real reason an entry stopped short, such as the partner cancelling, instead of always saying the window cap was reached.
    • Fixed · Bot: Ability checks know when you are dead or stunned. Abilities.CanCastOn now answers dead or stunned, and heroic opportunities and the ability query stop picking abilities then, honouring the two stun checkboxes as the cast stack does. The ability query also uses the same range, movement and position checks as everything else, and Lifeburn and Manaburn are no longer refused for the IsReady test the cast stack skips.
    • Fixed · Bot: Allow Specials to be Targets and Enable PvP now work. Both boxes now do what they say: a Special actor, or an enemy player, can be your kill target, so the Cast Stack, auto-attack and movement act on it. A few zones whose fights need a Special allow it with the box off, as classic OgreBot did. A shiny now also waits for your cast to finish before the bot goes for it.
    • Fixed · Bot: Cancelling a cast for a cure potion now drinks the potion. With X Abilities to Auto Cure Potions ticked, the bot cancels your cast and drinks the potion, even with the auto cure potion box off. Before, it could cancel cast after cast and never drink, and a potion on recast could read as ready. It leaves a cure on the bar alone, stops if the cast stack or its cures are switched off, and does nothing while paused or told not to cure.
    • Fixed · Bot: CastInterrupt keeps your cast when no interrupt is ready. Abilities:CastInterrupt looks for a ready interrupt first and cancels what you are casting only if it found one. Before, it dropped your cast and then found nothing to interrupt with, unlike the classic bot and the fight-file castInterrupt step.
    • Fixed · Bot: Fighters use a Formless Energy Inverter to dispel. A fighter holding a Handcrafted Formless Energy Inverter now uses it when a fight calls for a dispel. The bot searched for it in a way known to miss Formless items, so the dispel could stay silent, and a recast on it could read as ready. It now finds all five inverters the way the classic bot did.

Scripts can now see whether crafting is busy, which stage it is at, how far along it is (3 of 10), and whether the last queue was short on resources. Tick the new Session watch box on the EverQuest 2 tab of ogre config (off by default) and the uplink says in every OGRE console when a session goes silent for 30 seconds, comes back, or disappears, and fires an event your uplink scripts can act on. ogre sessions lists every session the uplink hears and when it was last heard; ogre sessions on|off turns the watch on or off until the uplink restarts. The uplink only announces; what to do about it is up to your script.

In zones with teleporters inside them, a run you start part-way (for example "start at named 3", or a resume) now begins only where the group is standing: at the start of a walk, at a named, or where the step before would have left it. Move to area works again without a target, and counting items in your bags now counts every stack and the Harvest Bag, by exact name or by part of the name.
